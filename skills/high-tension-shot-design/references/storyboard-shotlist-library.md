# Storyboard Shotlist Library

Use this when the user wants 分镜, shot list, a short video structure, or reusable visual sequences.

## Shot List Fields

For each shot, specify:

- shot size / 景别
- angle / 角度
- camera movement / 运镜
- subject action / 主体动作
- scene motion / 环境运动
- emotional function / 情绪功能
- duration / 时长

## Character Entrance

Shot 1:

- extreme wide or wide shot
- silhouette enters from strong negative space
- slow push-in or locked camera
- function: establish aura

Shot 2:

- low medium-full shot
- coat/hair/prop moves in foreground
- slight lateral track
- function: reveal design and power

Shot 3:

- close-up on eyes or hand
- no big motion
- function: lock identity and attitude

## Motorcycle / Vehicle Speed

Shot 1:

- insert shot, wheel/boot/key/handlebar
- tiny mechanical motion
- function: anticipation

Shot 2:

- low wide tracking shot
- vehicle crosses frame diagonally
- foreground parallax and road streaks
- function: speed release

Shot 3:

- rear 3/4 or side hero shot
- slow-motion hold after burst
- function: cool final image

## Duel / Confrontation

Shot 1:

- wide symmetrical or frame-within-frame shot
- both subjects separated by negative space
- function: establish power relation

Shot 2:

- over-the-shoulder or profile medium shot
- foreground subject blocks part of frame
- function: pressure and distance

Shot 3:

- extreme close-up on eye/finger/weapon edge
- function: decision point

## Emotional Rupture

Shot 1:

- locked medium shot
- subject still, environment subtle
- function: control

Shot 2:

- slow push-in to close-up
- micro-expression leaks out
- function: break

Shot 3:

- pull-out or negative-space wide
- subject becomes small
- function: aftermath

## Horror / Threat Reveal

Shot 1:

- long hallway / doorway / mirror frame
- locked camera
- function: dread

Shot 2:

- foreground wipe or slow lateral slide
- threat appears in negative space
- function: reveal

Shot 3:

- sudden close-up or smash cut
- function: shock

## Product / Object Power Shot

Shot 1:

- macro detail, material texture
- function: luxury and tactile detail

Shot 2:

- medium hero shot with strong reflection/shadow
- function: form reveal

Shot 3:

- overhead or blueprint-like shot
- function: design intelligence

## Prompt Block

```text
分镜采用[sequence type]结构：
1. [景别 + 角度 + 运镜 + 动作 + 功能]
2. [景别 + 角度 + 运镜 + 动作 + 功能]
3. [景别 + 角度 + 运镜 + 动作 + 功能]
整体保持同一视觉方向、同一光影逻辑、同一主体身份。每个镜头只承担一个信息功能。
```
