# Effect Vocabulary

Use this when the user asks for stronger effects, anime impact, speed, power, pressure, or commercial polish.

## Impact Effects

Impact frame:

- one-frame-like visual accent at the moment of hit/reveal
- high contrast silhouette, white flash, red/black inversion, hard shadow
- prompt: `brief impact-frame feeling at the peak moment, high contrast silhouette, sharp graphic shadow`

Speed ramp:

- slow -> fast -> slow rhythm
- prompt: `brief speed ramp: motion accelerates for the impact, then slows into a held final pose`

Directional motion blur:

- adds speed but must follow one clear direction
- prompt: `controlled directional motion blur trailing along the character's movement path`

Parallax:

- foreground moves faster than background, strong depth
- prompt: `foreground objects slide past the lens, background moves slower, strong parallax depth`

Lens compression:

- makes background feel close and oppressive
- prompt: `long-lens compression, background threat feels close behind the subject`

Wide-angle distortion:

- makes near objects larger, dynamic, aggressive
- prompt: `low wide-angle perspective, foreground hand/weapon/vehicle exaggerated toward camera`

Silhouette cut:

- creates myth, threat, power
- prompt: `hard backlight silhouette, only edge highlights and key details visible`

Foreground wipe:

- object passes in front of lens to transition or reveal
- prompt: `dark foreground shape wipes across the lens, revealing the subject behind it`

## Environment Tension

Use 1-2 only:

- rain streaks
- smoke layers
- dust motes
- sparks
- flickering fluorescent light
- drifting ash
- glass reflection
- moving shadow
- passing headlights
- steam burst
- curtain/cloth movement

Prompt:

```text
环境只做克制微动：[rain/smoke/sparks/shadow/headlights]，服务主体情绪，不抢戏。
```

## Anime / Graphic Impact

Useful terms:

- Obari-style heroic low angle / 大张力低机位
- speed lines, but controlled
- sharp rim light
- black shadow shapes
- high-contrast color block
- exaggerated foreshortening
- delayed follow-through
- held impact pose

Prompt:

```text
动漫冲击感来自低机位、强透视、剪影轮廓、硬边阴影和短暂冲击帧，不要堆满无意义速度线。
```

## Avoid

- excessive chromatic aberration
- random glitch effect
- overused lens flare
- too much smoke hiding the subject
- spark particles everywhere
- motion blur that destroys readability
- impact frame on every moment
