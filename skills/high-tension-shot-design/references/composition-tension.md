# Composition Tension

Use this when the user wants a still image, cover, keyframe, or first frame to feel more powerful.

## Tension Types

压迫感:

- low angle looking up
- subject fills frame, cropped close to edges
- deep shadows pressing from top or sides
- foreground obstruction partly blocks the viewer
- large architecture/object looming over small figure

孤独感:

- tiny subject in large negative space
- subject pushed to one side of frame
- empty foreground or dead space around the subject
- long lens compression with distant background
- shallow depth isolating subject from everyone else

危险感:

- diagonal composition
- dutch angle or tilted horizon
- sharp foreground shapes
- subject partly hidden by shadow, smoke, doorframe, glass, weapon, vehicle frame
- threat enters from negative space

史诗感:

- extreme wide shot
- vertical scale with tilt-up feeling
- foreground miniature human vs massive landscape/object
- backlight silhouette
- layered deep space: foreground, midground, far background all meaningful

亲密张力:

- close-up or extreme close-up
- only eyes, lips, fingers, or breath visible
- shallow depth of field
- faces close but not touching
- frame cuts off escape space

速度/冲击:

- low camera near ground
- wide-angle perspective stretch
- subject entering frame diagonally
- motion streaks or directional blur
- foreground streaks crossing frame

## Composition Devices

Use one or two at most:

- negative space as emotional pressure
- frame within frame: doorway, window, mirror, helmet visor, car window, weapon arc
- leading lines: road, rail, cables, architecture, blade edge, light beams
- center symmetry: ritual, control, authority, threat
- broken symmetry: rebellion, instability, hidden danger
- deep space: foreground object, midground subject, background threat
- extreme foreground: object close to lens, subject behind it
- off-center face: distrust, alienation, surveillance
- partial face crop: secrecy, aggression, psychological pressure
- silhouette: mythic power, anonymity, threat

## Prompt Patterns

Image prompt insert:

```text
构图采用[low angle / extreme wide / close-up / frame within frame]，主体被[负空间/前景遮挡/巨大背景/强对角线]压住，画面主次极清晰，只有一个视觉焦点。使用[深阴影/逆光/浅景深/长焦压缩/广角透视]强化[压迫/孤独/速度/危险/亲密]。
```

Video first-frame insert:

```text
首帧保持强构图：主体位于[位置]，前景有[遮挡物]，背景形成[线条/巨大空间/负空间]，画面从一开始就有[压迫/孤独/危险]感。
```

## Avoid

- every object competing for attention
- decorative diagonals with no story purpose
- centered subject plus random clutter
- low angle on weak subjects unless the scene means irony or false power
- too much text explaining the tension inside the image
