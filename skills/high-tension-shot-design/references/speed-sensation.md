# Speed Sensation

Use this when the user wants 速度感, 爆发力, chase energy, vehicle motion, fast action, or a shot that feels faster without becoming unreadable.

## Core Principle

Speed is not just "fast movement." It comes from contrast:

- stillness before motion
- low camera near the ground
- clear movement direction
- foreground parallax
- short hold at the impact point
- environment reacting to motion
- fast/slow rhythm shift

## Visual Mechanics

Low ground tracking:

- Best for vehicles, running, skating, motorcycle, animal chase.
- Prompt: `low ground-level tracking shot, camera close to the road, foreground streaks rush past, subject cuts diagonally through frame`.

Foreground parallax:

- Best for speed while keeping subject readable.
- Prompt: `near foreground objects whip past the lens while the subject remains sharp, strong parallax layers`.

Diagonal entry:

- Best for aggressive action and forward thrust.
- Prompt: `subject enters from lower-left toward upper-right, strong diagonal movement path, frame feels sliced open`.

Long-lens compression chase:

- Best for danger closing in.
- Prompt: `telephoto compression, background vehicle/threat feels dangerously close behind the subject`.

Wide-angle thrust:

- Best for heroic/chaotic speed.
- Prompt: `wide-angle low camera, front wheel/hand/weapon enlarged near lens, depth stretches behind the subject`.

Motion blur:

- Use directional blur around edges and background, not on the face/identity anchor.
- Prompt: `controlled directional motion blur in the background and foreground, subject's face/identity remains readable`.

Speed ramp:

- Use slow -> fast -> slow: anticipation, burst, pose/impact.
- Prompt: `speed ramp feeling: brief slow anticipation, sudden acceleration through the action, then a held final pose`.

Shutter smear / anime smear:

- Use for stylized anime action.
- Prompt: `one clean anime smear frame along the movement path, graphic but readable, no random speed-line clutter`.

## Vehicle Speed Formula

```text
低机位贴近地面，镜头与车辆并行跟拍，前景护栏/灯牌/雨水快速掠过形成强视差，车身保持清晰，轮胎光环和尾灯拖出方向性光迹，最后落在一个稳定的帅气侧后方构图。
```

## Human Action Speed Formula

```text
动作开始前短暂停顿，手/脚/武器先进入画面，主体沿强对角线爆发，背景产生方向性运动模糊，冲击瞬间短暂定格，尘土/火花/衣料继续运动。
```

## Slow Speed Tension

Slow can feel powerful when:

- camera pushes in
- subject barely moves
- environment reacts subtly
- cut duration stretches beyond expectation

Prompt:

```text
动作极慢但压迫：主体几乎不动，只有眼神/手指/衣料微动，镜头缓慢推进，背景声音般的环境微动让时间被拉长。
```

## Avoid

- every part of the image blurred
- subject identity destroyed by speed
- fast camera plus fast subject plus fast background
- speed lines covering the action
- random sparks with no contact point
- camera spinning when the motion direction should be clear
