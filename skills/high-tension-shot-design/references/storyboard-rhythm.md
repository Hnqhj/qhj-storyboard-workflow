# Storyboard Rhythm

Use this when the user asks for 分镜, 3秒/5秒/8秒/15秒视频, action beats, reveal structure, or stronger pacing.

## One-Shot 5-Second Structure

Best for image-to-video:

```text
0-1s: hold the original composition, let the viewer read the subject.
1-3s: one main movement begins.
3-4s: visual pressure increases: camera, environment, or object reaction.
4-5s: settle on a readable emotional/image endpoint.
```

Use only one main action. Add at most one environmental secondary motion.

## Three-Shot Tension Structure

For a short sequence:

镜头1：建立危险/欲望/距离

- wide or medium shot
- strong geometry or negative space
- clear subject relation

镜头2：压缩信息

- close-up or object detail
- hand, eye, weapon, reflection, screen, door gap
- tension becomes specific

镜头3：释放/反转/落点

- impact, reveal, decision, turn, silence after movement
- one memorable final frame

## Action Beat Structure

起势:

- low angle / close detail / stillness before motion
- breath, grip, foot shift, eye movement

爆发:

- diagonal motion, whip pan, motion blur, impact frame, foreground streak

收势:

- locked frame, dust/sparks/rain continues, subject holds final silhouette

## Emotional Beat Structure

触发:

- subject sees/hears/realizes something

压住:

- micro-expression, fingers tighten, gaze avoids, body barely moves

泄露:

- one tiny action betrays the emotion

落点:

- camera holds, emotion settles, viewer understands what changed

## Prompt Template

```text
分镜节奏：先[建立/触发]，再[压缩/爆发]，最后[收势/落点]。每个镜头只承担一个信息功能。避免连续堆动作，避免每一秒都在变。
```

## Avoid

- every shot being close-up
- every shot moving fast
- unclear subject goal
- adding a new object/character every beat
- unresolved final frame
