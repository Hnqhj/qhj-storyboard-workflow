# Kinetic Action Master Language

## Source Principle

A successful opening paragraph can dominate the model's interpretation of every later shot. Its job is not to enumerate events. Its job is to establish a stable high-energy visual prior: what kind of image this is, how far the camera may depart from convention, how animation exaggeration coexists with realism, and which forms of chaos are forbidden.

Named directors, studios, films, visual schools, genres, and martial or dance systems can be stronger than long descriptive substitutions because they act as compact semantic priors. Keep a deliberate user-selected name in the prompt. The mechanism language below is support and diagnosis, not a mandatory replacement.

Use this compression order:

```text
named anchor -> optional short disambiguation -> physical/spatial guardrail
```

Examples:

```text
大张正己式动作张力、扳机社式高速动画爆发；强化夸张透视与冲击帧，但保持真人电影级材质和空间连续。
卡波耶拉动作基底；只有在需要时再补“持续摇摆、低位偏轴、圆弧路线”，不要把流派名删掉。
```

## Anchor-To-Mechanism Support Map

| Compact anchor | Optional support mechanism |
|---|---|
| IMAX-level live-action quality | large-format sense of scale; deep spatial layering; controlled highlight roll-off; readable shadow detail; realistic surface response; selective depth of field; natural motion blur |
| strongly stylized | decisive silhouette; controlled palette; asymmetric composition; graphic light-dark separation; repeatable shape and motion language |
| anime studio/director energy | extreme foreshortening; explosive pose transitions; dynamic stretch; speed smears; brief graphic impact frames; abrupt but motivated scale changes |
| POV | short subjective or body-adjacent passages; near-lens foreground; visible target relation; clean entry and exit to an objective anchor |
| unconventional composition | foreground obstruction; edge cropping; negative-space imbalance; axial staging; severe high/low angle; off-center power relation |
| violent/free camera | action-led tracking; whip reframe; rapid push/pull; short arc; short controlled 45–90° partial roll; occlusion or weapon-path cut |
| many shots and many shot sizes | functional ladder from insert/extreme close-up to full body and extreme wide; every cut adds information or changes power/scale |
| fast and overwhelming | dense but hierarchical information; stable establishing anchor; escalating disruption; readable contact; resolved final state |
| strict physics plus exaggeration | real support, center path, traction, inertia, recoil, reaction, braking, and recovery beneath stylized timing and pose design |
| no distortion or collapse | persistent anatomy, limb count, joint direction, identity, costume, weapon form, contact geometry, and stable scene topology |

## Nine Control Layers

### 1. Image And Material

Define medium, surface truth, light hierarchy, shadow detail, highlight behavior, atmospheric depth, and optical motion behavior. “High quality” and “8K” are not substitutes.

### 2. Composition

Choose a recurring family:

- diagonal pressure and asymmetry;
- foreground rupture and occlusion;
- axial depth and direct approach;
- scale mismatch between figure and environment;
- negative-space compression or release.

Do not activate every family in every beat.

### 3. Camera

Choose two to four permitted behaviors for the whole clip:

- ground-skimming pursuit;
- body-adjacent over-shoulder or brief POV;
- short arc around a contact point;
- rapid push/pull tied to acceleration or revelation;
- whip reframe following a weapon or gaze;
- occlusion cut through a body, weapon, debris, wall, or light pulse;
- short partial roll motivated by a flip, fall, impact, or orientation change.

Camera energy must answer: what moved, what crossed the lens, what changed in space, or why the editor cut.

### 4. Expressive Multi-Shot Storyboard Grammar

Use special storyboard expression to turn one physical action phrase into several visually different but causally connected views.

Each shot should change at least two useful dimensions:

- function: orientation, intent, mechanism, path, contact, reaction, consequence;
- shot size: extreme close-up, insert, close, medium, full body, wide, extreme wide;
- camera height/angle: ground level, low, eye level, high oblique, overhead, axial, body-adjacent POV;
- depth relation: foreground rupture, profile plane, deep axial space, overhead map, compressed telephoto layers;
- viewpoint relation: objective anchor, performer-adjacent, target-adjacent, weapon/body POV, environmental witness.

Connect the chain through:

- continuous body or weapon vector;
- the same action phase progressing across the cut;
- stable screen direction and axis;
- eye trace or target placement;
- foreground object, silhouette, shape, light, or sound inherited by the next shot;
- visible change in distance, level, balance, initiative, weapon state, or environment.

The goal is not merely “many shots.” It is high contrast between views with low confusion between actions.

### 5. Shot Scale

Use a functional range:

- extreme close-up: decision, grip, eye, trigger, lock, fracture;
- close/medium: body intention, weapon control, reaction;
- full body: support, path, leverage, recovery;
- wide/extreme wide: geography, scale, displacement, consequence.

Avoid consecutive shots that preserve the same scale, height, path, and information.

### 6. Action-Camera Coupling

The performer and camera should form one visual mechanism:

- explosive forward commitment may trigger a ground-skimming retreat, axial push, or near-lens pass;
- circular or inverted movement may trigger a short arc or partial roll that follows the body's real orientation change;
- heavy impact may briefly resist camera motion, then release into recoil or a wider consequence view;
- a weapon/body crossing the lens may create an occlusion cut into the next action phase;
- the camera may oppose the movement to increase compression, or travel with it to preserve velocity.

Camera behavior must expose a pose, trajectory, support change, contact result, or recovery. It must not perform an unrelated stunt while the character executes generic movement.

For exchanges, use camera tracking ownership:

```text
initiator owns the lens during setup and commitment
-> body or weapon briefly outruns the lens
-> lens decelerates at the contact plane
-> receiver owns the lens during recoil, fall, slide, or redirected movement
-> wider frame proves the new spacing
```

Keep lens speed below the leading action layer. Use short arcs to reveal contact geometry and let body-level changes motivate camera height. Avoid continuous orbit around a centered performer.

### 7. Motion And Animation

Permit:

- forceful anticipation silhouettes;
- brief dynamic stretch during acceleration;
- directional speed smears;
- one- or two-frame graphic impact accents;
- pose-to-pose contrast;
- fast/slow/fast rhythm;
- controlled motion blur with a readable leading edge.

Never use these to hide missing contact or incorrect anatomy.

### 8. Effects And Transitions

Use this causal structure:

```text
outgoing action/effect source
-> frame coverage, matched vector, shape, color, light, material, or sound
-> incoming shot inherits that element
-> new geography, information, or state becomes readable
```

Useful families:

- **match on action**: a hand, body, weapon, wheel, or creature limb continues the same movement across the cut;
- **vector match**: outgoing left-to-right or circular motion becomes an incoming motion with the same direction;
- **occlusion wipe**: body, weapon, cloth, architecture, debris, smoke, shadow, or foreground object fills the frame and reveals the next view;
- **shape/material match**: blade arc becomes architecture, eye becomes core, circular shield becomes moon or mechanism, debris becomes rain or dust;
- **light-state bridge**: impact white, blackout, scan line, muzzle light, reflection, or shadow crossing carries into the next lighting state;
- **sound bridge**: J-cut, L-cut, impact resonance, breath, machinery, music transient, or silence prepares or trails the image cut;
- **effect-path bridge**: a sourced cable, energy line, slash trail, dust wave, water sheet, or spark path crosses the frame and continues as a real element in the next shot.

Choose two or three transition families for one short film. Repeat them with variation so they become part of the visual language.

Transitions should do one of three jobs:

1. preserve momentum;
2. compress time or distance;
3. announce a real change in power, weapon state, location, viewpoint, or emotional meaning.

Do not use a transition to hide the exact contact, reaction, braking, or consequence the viewer needs to understand.

For transformation effects:

```text
old state
-> visible active frontier
-> completed new state
```

Keep all three states visible together when possible. Move the frontier through named regions, assign different exit/reveal/assembly behavior to each material, and reserve silhouette-defining structures for the completion phase. A flash or occlusion may bridge one difficult topology change only briefly; the next image must restore identity, background, and connected structure.

### 8.5 Density Envelope

Do not keep the full film at one cut rate.

```text
longer orientation and ability ownership
-> moderate acceleration
-> compressed exchange or transformation threshold
-> brief decisive peak
-> longer consequence and settling
```

Use exact macro timing only when evidence, music, or physical phase budgeting supports it. Do not force equal-duration action nodes merely to fill the runtime.

### 9. Physics And Coherence

Preserve:

- support foot or contact anchor;
- center-of-mass path;
- weapon or body mass behavior;
- reaction in both participants;
- environmental force transfer;
- inertia continuation;
- braking and stable end state;
- axis, screen direction, target relation, and location topology.

## Paste-Ready Universal Template

```text
【高能动作视觉母版】
整片采用顶级IMAX级真人电影质感与高度风格化动画动力学融合；保留用户指定的导演、动画公司、电影或视觉流派名称作为首要风格锚点，例如“大张正己式动作张力、扳机社式高速动画爆发”。真实可信的皮肤、织物、金属、建筑与环境光影，宽容的高光过渡、保留细节的深阴影、清楚的空间层次和尺度；画面允许强透视、夸张缩短、动态拉伸、方向明确的速度涂抹与短促高反差冲击帧，但人物结构、支撑、重心、惯性、接触反作用、制动与收势始终成立。

镜头语言激进但有因果：采用特殊的分镜表现手法，通过多分镜、多景别、多特殊角度实现动作的衔接和切换；景别、机位高度、透视、前后景关系和主客观视点大幅变化，但相邻镜头继承同一动作矢量、动作阶段、眼动落点、轴线和空间因果。打破居中和平视构图，使用近景遮挡、前景破框、轴向冲刺、贴地跟拍、短促主观或越肩视角、强透视、快速推拉、动作匹配切镜，以及由翻转、坠落或冲击触发的短促受控45–90°局部滚转。

人物动作极度夸张、灵巧、生动而富有张力；身体姿态、动作路线、加速、翻转、接触和收势主动创造镜头机会，镜头则通过追随、迎击、反向压缩、环绕、掠镜和景别突变强化同一动作，人物与镜头共同完成视觉爆发。景别从极端特写、局部插入、中近景、全身动作到巨尺度大全景大幅跳变，相邻镜头必须改变功能、尺度、信息或力量关系。转场继承上一镜的动作方向、武器掠镜、前景遮挡、形状、材质、光线或声音。整体动感凌厉、逻辑清晰、高密度而有序；始终看清主体轮廓、动作路径、接触结果、敌我反应、空间位移与最终稳定状态。禁止多镜头各自独立摆拍、景别变化没有信息差、特殊角度破坏动作方向、随机甩镜、相机代替人物运动、剪辑遮盖动作、特效遮住肢体和武器路径、人体扭曲、身份或武器漂移、动作轴线跳变、背景拓扑重建。
```

## Adaptation Knobs

### Duel Or Group Combat

Add body/weapon path readability, screen-direction ownership, relay order, approach-contact-reaction-displacement, and distinct movement grammar per performer.

### Giant Or Mecha Combat

Prioritize extreme scale alternation, ground and architecture response, delayed mass motion, long stopping distance, debris with a physical source, and wide shots after impact to prove displacement.

### Weapon Or Ability Showcase

Prioritize grip, deployment, mass distribution, full-body support, trajectory, sourced transitions, chapter contrast, and a final icon. Do not invent an opponent merely to create energy.

### Chase Or Vehicle Action

Prioritize directional continuity, foreground parallax, road/terrain response, suspension or gait, relative speed, braking distance, and brief objective anchors between subjective bursts.

## Failure Corrections

- **Result is chaotic:** reduce the permitted camera family to two movements; add stable master anchors before and after the peak.
- **Result is energetic but generic:** define the performer's movement grammar and the camera's relationship to that grammar.
- **Cuts feel random:** attach every cut to gaze, weapon path, occlusion, impact, direction match, or information reveal.
- **Transitions feel like templates:** remove unrelated glitches, portals, and flashes; carry a real outgoing action, material, light, shape, or sound into the incoming shot.
- **Action is unreadable:** restore full-body support shots and post-impact wide shots; shorten POV and effect coverage.
- **Looks like a game trailer rather than cinema:** retain the chosen cinematic anchor, then add material-light behavior, lens depth, restrained effects, and longer consequence shots.
- **Looks realistic but dull:** increase silhouette contrast, shot-scale jumps, foreground rupture, foreshortening, and pose-to-pose timing.
- **Looks animated but weightless:** strengthen support, traction, recoil, follow-through, stopping distance, and environmental force transfer.
- **Camera is doing the fighting:** redesign the action basis first, then let the camera reveal or amplify it.
