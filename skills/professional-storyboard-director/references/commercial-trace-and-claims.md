# Commercial Causality, Treatment Trace and Claim Carriers

## Work-type gate

Choose before outlining shots:

- **product causal film:** `specific obstacle → trigger → product intervention → observable proof → changed state → brand payoff`;
- **product-state beauty/packshot:** product start state, material/light/motion change, readable result and memory frame;
- **sensory PV:** motif, scale/rhythm progression and payoff without pretending it proves a feature;
- **full narrative:** character causality plus only the approved product/brand role.

Run the product-removal test. If the plot survives unchanged, the product is not the causal engine. Do not force a fake human problem into a legitimate beauty or material film.

## Treatment-to-shot trace

```text
treatment rule | scope | controlled shots | prompt fields | acceptance evidence | status
```

Forward audit every approved rule into shot evidence. Reverse-audit every hero, proof and transition shot back to a brief source, approved rule or labelled proposal. A rule with no shot is an orphan rule; a shot with no source is an orphan shot.

## Claim ledger

```text
claim | source/version | evidence grade | approval | allowed carrier | disclosure | shots/clocks
```

At minimum distinguish verified released behavior, prototype/research demo, dramatized reconstruction, inference/analogy and brand declaration. Allowed carriers may include observable picture proof, verified UI/data, authorized VO/dialogue/interview, Super and disclosure.

Generated imagery must not upgrade evidence. A prototype cannot be shown as unlabelled everyday released behavior; a disclaimer cannot repair a fundamentally misleading hero image. This ledger organizes evidence and does not replace legal or client approval.

## Storyboard output additions

For commercial shots add only when relevant:

```text
shot job / causal role / claim ID-grade / approved carrier / proof window / changed state / acceptance evidence / fallback
```
