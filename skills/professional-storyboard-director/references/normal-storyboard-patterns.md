# Normal Storyboard Patterns

Use these when the scene needs professional clarity rather than maximum visual violence.

## Dialogue And Emotional Scenes

Core ladder:

```text
relationship geography
-> listener behavior
-> speaker pressure
-> withheld reaction or object detail
-> changed distance, posture, or silence
```

Useful shots:

- master two-shot to define distance and power;
- over-shoulder for pressure or alignment;
- clean single for inner decision;
- insert for residue: phone, glass, ring, wound, message, unfinished task;
- reaction shot after the line, not always during the line.
- J-cut/L-cut or sound bridge when the listener's reaction, hesitation, or offscreen pressure matters more than seeing the speaker finish the line.

Avoid cutting only on dialogue lines. Cut on listening, realization, avoidance, interruption, or changed power.
Do not turn dialogue into mechanical speaker coverage. Use the listener's face, hands, posture, silence, or delayed answer as story action when it carries the scene's real change.

## Quiet Drama

Show emotion through time-state evidence:

- just happened: residue on body, prop, room, weather, screen, or sound;
- happening now: small physical task or hesitation;
- about to happen: incoming pressure, warning cue, shadow, notification, footstep, engine, door;
- consequence: changed posture, abandoned object, stopped sound, altered route.

Use stillness as active composition: negative space, blocked eyeline, asymmetry, distance, repeated shape, or sound contrast.

## Product, Prop, And Brand

Core ladder:

```text
context of need
-> object scale and silhouette
-> hand/use relation
-> material or mechanism proof
-> result or memory frame
```

Shots must answer:

- what is it;
- how big is it;
- how does it feel or function;
- what changes because of it;
- what frame should remain in memory.

Avoid empty beauty shots without usage, scale, or consequence.

## Travel, Place, And World

Core ladder:

```text
place identity
-> route or access
-> human scale
-> texture/detail
-> turn or reveal
-> final relationship to the place
```

Vary wide orientation, human-scale medium, detail insert, moving viewpoint, and final held frame.

## Comedy Or Light Scenes

Clarity beats style:

- establish expectation;
- hold long enough for behavior;
- cut to reveal contradiction;
- preserve reaction timing;
- avoid overcutting before the joke lands.

## Exposition

Make information physical:

- use maps, screens, props, routes, hands, labels, or environmental changes only when they are visible story actions;
- use visual comparison instead of verbal summary;
- cut from abstract information to human consequence.
