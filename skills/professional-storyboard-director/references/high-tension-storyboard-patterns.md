# High-Tension Storyboard Patterns

Use these when the brief asks for high pressure, danger, speed, power, dread, awe, action, or memorable impact.

## Tension Sources

Pick the dominant source before designing shots:

- proximity: subject too close to threat, lens, edge, weapon, vehicle, wall, crowd, or deadline;
- scale: small body against huge space, creature, machine, architecture, army, storm, or system;
- occlusion: partial view, foreground wipe, smoke, rain, glass, body, cloth, debris, shadow;
- delay: viewer sees warning before subject, or subject sees too late;
- compression: telephoto stacking, narrowing route, collapsing distance;
- instability: tilted horizon, handheld stress, rolling frame, subjective POV, but only when motivated;
- rupture: one sudden break after a held composition;
- consequence: reaction, damage, displacement, silence, or new spatial state.

## High-Tension Sequence Ladder

```text
pressure hook
-> geography proof
-> intent or vulnerability
-> obstacle enters
-> acceleration or reveal
-> impact/near miss/choice
-> consequence
-> release, inversion, or loop
```

For AI video, compress this to 5-7 beats for 15 seconds. Do not create a dozen tiny cuts unless the model and prompt can support it.

## Dominant Devices

Rotate devices across the sequence:

- negative-space trap: subject pinned to frame edge while empty or threatening space dominates;
- worm's-eye pressure: low camera makes body, machine, or architecture overrun the viewer;
- overhead map reveal: route, trap, formation, or crowd logic becomes visible;
- foreground rupture: weapon, wheel, hand, debris, cloth, or light crosses close to lens for one instant;
- telephoto crush: obstacles stack, reducing escape space;
- subjective delay: viewer sees the danger via reflection, shadow, screen, or sound before direct view;
- scale cut: tiny subject in huge space followed by close detail of strain;
- silence break: hold a still frame, then cut or move on a sound/impact cue;
- contact proof: show support, force line, contact, receiver response, and changed spacing;
- aftermath hold: the strongest proof is the quiet changed state after motion.

## Action And Combat

The camera must reveal the action, not replace it.

Use this chain:

```text
intent detail
-> full-body route or support proof
-> acceleration angle
-> contact or near-miss proof
-> receiver/environment consequence
-> recovery or changed spacing
```

Rules:

- keep a current camera tracking owner;
- transfer tracking only when initiative or force transfers;
- show feet/support for heavy movement;
- at contact, briefly calm the camera;
- do not cut away before reaction or displacement;
- use close-ups for grip, foot brace, breath, eyes, trigger, blade bind, cable tension, or material compression.

## Horror, Dread, And Psychological Pressure

Do not make dread fast by default.

Useful structure:

```text
normal frame with wrong detail
-> delayed recognition
-> negative space grows
-> sound or shadow confirms pressure
-> subject reacts late
-> frame holds after the threat changes state
```

Use locked camera, slow push, offscreen sound, blocked eyeline, deep background movement, or reflection. Use distortion sparingly.

## Chase, Vehicle, And Speed

Speed needs readable layer separation:

- stable subject silhouette;
- faster foreground streaks;
- background parallax;
- route obstacle;
- scale proof;
- one peak rupture;
- consequence frame.

Avoid only side tracking. Mix low/wide, overhead route, telephoto compression, interior POV, foreground pass, and wide consequence.

## Speed Without Spatial Collapse

For chase, sprint, vehicle, flight, fall, or high-speed combat, lock a readable attention path before adding camera aggression:

- choose one eye-trace anchor per beat: face, weapon tip, vehicle nose, route exit, landmark, impact point, or pursued target;
- establish route geometry before acceleration: start point, screen direction, obstacle line, destination, and one geography reset option;
- keep screen direction stable across the fast section unless the axis break is motivated by a clear overhead, wide, or landmark reset;
- create speed through layer contrast: stable subject or silhouette, fast foreground pass, slower background parallax, and one readable obstacle;
- after close-ups, heavy occlusion, subjective POV, whip pan, or impact blur, reset geography before the next maneuver;
- at near-miss or contact, briefly simplify the camera so cause, contact/avoidance, and consequence can be read;
- for AI video, use one dominant motion vector and one dominant camera move per generated segment; do not combine orbit, whip pan, crash zoom, handheld shake, speed blur, and complex blocking in the same segment.

## Transformation Or Power Reveal

Every change needs a source:

- hinge, rail, seam, joint, latch, nested segment, light frontier, skin/material boundary, cable, compression, unfolding surface.

Storyboard the frontier:

```text
before state
-> trigger detail
-> active changing edge
-> partial completed form beside old surface
-> mechanical/physical lock
-> new silhouette in context
```
