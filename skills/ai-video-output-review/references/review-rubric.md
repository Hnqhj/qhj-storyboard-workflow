# AI Video Output Review Rubric

Use only dimensions relevant to the intended clip. A visible observation outranks an inferred metric.

## 1. Delivery Integrity

- File opens and decodes.
- Duration, aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and audio presence match the target.
- No unintended black frames, frozen tail, corrupted frames, watermark, subtitle, or text.

## 2. Prompt And Beat Adherence

- The required subject, action, location, prop, and ending image appear.
- Timed beats occur in the intended order.
- The first frame establishes the promised state; the ending pays off or loops as intended.

## 3. Subject Identity, Count, And Structure

- Same protagonist, face, body proportions, costume, hair, makeup, and age.
- Correct number of characters and objects.
- No duplicate protagonist, extra limbs, merged bodies, disappearing prop, or unrequested replacement.

## 4. Spatial Continuity And Blocking

- Left/right, foreground/background, distance, scale, facing, gaze, action path, and long-axis orientation remain coherent.
- Camera changes do not rebuild the stage or violate the axis without motivation.
- Contact points between body, vehicle, ground, weapon, and environment are readable.

## 5. Motion And Temporal Consistency

- Motion has anticipation, acceleration, peak, follow-through, and recovery when appropriate.
- No temporal flicker, texture boiling, rubber limbs, sliding feet, unstable silhouette, repeated loop, or frozen pose.
- Motion blur preserves the hero silhouette and blurs only intended layers.

## 6. Camera And Editing

- Every shot or camera phase has a function.
- Shot size, angle, height, movement, and cut reason support information and emotion.
- Screen direction and eyelines remain readable.
- Camera motion does not repeat, conflict, or conceal the main action.

## 7. Physical And Causal Plausibility

- Weight, inertia, traction, recoil, collision, deformation, water, cloth, hair, debris, and environmental reaction match the action.
- Actor strength is consistent relative to the body, weapon, vehicle, or creature load.
- Weapon mass distribution remains stable: grip-biased, balanced, tip-heavy, head-heavy, or flexible-delayed behavior does not drift between shots.
- The projected center of mass remains supported, or a visible step, slide, fall, brace, cable, wall, vehicle, powered assist, or airborne phase resolves it.
- Startup, contact, follow-through, braking, and recovery fit the mass and length.
- Contact produces equal-and-opposite body/weapon response and a changed spatial state.
- Transformed forms show regripping, rebalancing, settling, and new handling behavior.
- Effects originate from a visible source and occupy the intended depth layer.
- No line, projectile, shadow, wheel, or body intersects the wrong plane.

## 8. Style, Material, Light, And Optics

- Medium and art-direction rules remain stable.
- Character, environment, props, and effects share one rendering world.
- Roughness, reflection, anisotropy, subsurface response, AO/contact shadow, bounce light, atmosphere, lens behavior, depth of field, and shutter feel serve visible surfaces.
- No sudden switch from 2.5D/NPR to flat cartoon or unrelated photorealism.

## 9. Story, Performance, And Emotion

- Want, obstacle, choice, consequence, reveal, or micro-story function is legible.
- Expression and behavior evolve from visible temporal cause rather than random posing.
- Hook, turn, and payoff are proportionate to the duration.

## 10. Music, Sound, And Audio-Visual Relation

- Dialogue, music, ambience, foley, impacts, vehicle/weapon sound, and silence have clear priority.
- Sound perspective follows camera distance and space.
- Major sync points support action without mechanically hitting every cut.
- No missing source sound, clipped audio, obvious desynchronization, or music masking the narrative focus.

## Severity

- **Blocking**: makes the clip unusable or changes its meaning.
- **Major**: materially weakens continuity, action, story, style, or professional delivery.
- **Minor**: visible but does not prevent use.
- **Preference**: taste choice, not a defect; label it as such.

## Confidence

- **High**: directly visible or measurable in the artifact.
- **Medium**: strong inference supported by prompt/reference comparison.
- **Low**: cause cannot be isolated because inputs/settings are missing.
