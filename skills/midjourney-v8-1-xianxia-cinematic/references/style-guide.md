# Xianxia Cinematic Style Guide

## Core look

Create a live-action photorealistic film still rooted in Chinese xianxia and guofeng fantasy. Keep the result ethereal but physically believable.

## Wardrobe and materials

Use translucent layered silk, flowing sleeves and robes, refined Chinese hair ornaments, and jade, pearl, or gold details. When the subject is not human, adapt these material cues gracefully to its form.

## Light and palette

Use natural golden backlight, peach and ivory highlights, amber skin tones, optional teal-blue shadows, atmospheric haze, and restrained bloom.

## Composition and camera

Choose a narrative close-up through a wide establishing shot. Layer foreground fabric or mist, use shallow depth of field for portraits, and describe only realistic lens language.

## Style boundary

Exclude anime linework, painterly brushwork, watercolor wash, plastic 3D surfaces, cyberpunk technology, and Western medieval armor or castles.
