# Source Map

Research sources used to shape this skill. Use these as source anchors, not as verbatim prompt text.

## Chinese Audiovisual-Language Course Structure

- 中国大学MOOC《视听语言》: frames audiovisual language as a discipline spanning script, storyboard, shooting, editing, image, sound, and editing analysis.
  - https://www.icourse163.org/course/detail.htm?cid=1452331161
- 广东工业大学《视听语言》课程教学大纲: lists key components including shot, composition, shot size, angle, movement, axis, editing, and sound.
  - https://courses.gdut.edu.cn/mod/resource/view.php?id=76737
- 浙江传媒学院《中国大学MOOC / 影视视听语言》: course outline includes frame/composition, shot size/angle, camera movement, focal length/depth, lighting/color, staging, and sound-image construction.
  - https://www.icourse163.org/learn/ZJICM-1461020173
- 国家高等教育智慧教育平台《数字视频设计与制作技术》: course outline includes visual composition, shot size, angle, direction, focal length, shot length, camera movement, push/pull/pan/track/lift, sound, production, editing, and effects.
  - https://higher.smartedu.cn/course/67aa8ad7225d72705e095495

## Film Grammar And Cinematography

- StudioBinder, `50+ Types of Camera Shots, Angles, and Techniques`: used for shot-size/angle/movement categories and the principle that shot size, angle, framing, movement, lens, composition, and depth shape viewer perception.
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-camera-shots/
- StudioBinder, `Definitive Guide to Every Type of Camera Movement in Film`: used for camera movement categories and planning logic.
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/different-types-of-camera-movements-in-film/
- StudioBinder, `Mise en Scene`: used for mise-en-scene as the arrangement of what is in front of the camera.
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/mise-en-scene/

## Continuity And Axis

- Filmmakers Academy, `Axis Of Action`: used for axis-of-action / 180-degree line and screen-direction continuity.
  - https://www.filmmakersacademy.com/glossary/axis-of-action/
- Filmmakers Academy, `Eyeline Match`: used for eyeline match as a continuity-editing technique.
  - https://www.filmmakersacademy.com/glossary/eyeline-match/
- College Film & Media Studies, `Editing`: used for match-on-action as continuity editing.
  - https://collegefilmandmediastudies.com/editing/

## Sound And Rhythm

- Adobe, `Diegetic vs. non-diegetic sound in film`: used for diegetic/non-diegetic sound definitions and creative blending.
  - https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/hub/ideas/diegetic-vs-non-diegetic-sound.html
- Introduction to Film, `Sound`: used for sound bridge, J-cut, L-cut, sound perspective, subjective sound, dialogue overlap, and music rhythm.
  - https://introduction-to-film.com/sound/
