# Sound And Rhythm

Use this when designing pacing, cuts, impact beats, music, silence, or sound-image relation.

## Sound Types

- Diegetic sound: sound from inside the story world; characters could hear it.
- Non-diegetic sound: score, narration, or effect added for the audience.
- Off-screen sound: diegetic sound whose source is not visible in frame.
- Sound perspective: volume/tone changes to imply distance or subjectivity.
- Subjective sound: sound altered to reflect a character's perception or mental state.

## Sound-Edit Tools

- J-cut: hear the next shot before seeing it.
- L-cut: keep sound from the previous shot over the next image.
- Sound bridge: diegetic sound links two scenes.
- Dialogue overlap: dialogue crosses a cut within a scene.
- Motif: repeated sound gains meaning.
- Silence drop: remove sound just before impact so the hit feels sharper.

## Rhythm Is Contrast

Speed feels fast because something was slower before it. Impact feels heavy because there is a hold before or after it.

Useful structure for 15 seconds:

```text
0-2s: orientation or charged stillness
2-5s: first decisive motion
5-8s: acceleration or complication
8-11s: obstacle, reversal, or scale shift
11-13s: peak impact or reveal
13-15s: release, aftermath, or exit
```

Do not treat this as a fixed template. Use it as a rhythm sanity check.

## Cut Rhythm Patterns

Accelerating entrance:

```text
long wide -> medium tracking -> short insert -> short close-up -> held hero pass
```

Chase escalation:

```text
map wide -> ground-level thrust -> side tracking -> obstacle insert -> overhead update -> near miss -> exit wide
```

Shock:

```text
quiet hold -> sudden sound cue -> smash cut -> brief silence -> reaction close-up
```

Luxury/fashion:

```text
slow reveal -> material insert -> full silhouette -> controlled movement -> face close-up -> graphic end frame
```

## AI Prompt Sound Language

Even if the tool does not generate audio, sound words guide visual rhythm:

```text
engine whine rises, sound drops for one impact frame, then the city rush returns
```

Use sound cues to anchor cuts:

```text
cut on the wheel hitting the ramp
cut as the cyan light streak wipes the frame
J-cut with distant engine before the rider appears
```
