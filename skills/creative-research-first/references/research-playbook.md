# Creative Research Playbook

## Question Template

Before searching, ask:

1. What does the real subject/process sound, look, move, or behave like?
2. Which examples solve the exact creative problem?
3. Which professional terms give controllable results?
4. What current platform limits or controls affect feasibility?
5. What cultural, historical, legal, or safety boundary matters?

## Query Packs

Domain truth:

```text
[subject/process] official documentation mechanism materials workflow
[craft/profession] terminology technique educational institution
```

Visual mechanics:

```text
[shot/composition/motion problem] cinematography case study
[material/object] lighting photography reference
```

Music and sound:

```text
[scene function] film scoring spotting motif orchestration
[sound source] foley sound design perspective material
```

Current model/platform:

```text
site:official-domain [model] prompt reference duration aspect ratio audio
```

## Source Hierarchy

Prefer:

1. official documentation and primary sources;
2. universities, museums, standards bodies, and established training institutions;
3. direct practitioner interviews and studio breakdowns;
4. credible trade publications;
5. community examples for discovery only.

Use community sources as behavioral evidence, not official capability claims.


## Synthesis Test

Before prompt writing:

- Did research alter at least one important decision?
- Are references translated into mechanics?
- Is current platform guidance separated from timeless craft knowledge?
- Is the result original rather than a stitched imitation?
- Can unnecessary source detail be removed from the final prompt?
