# Xianxia And Period Scene Contract

Use this reference for Chinese historical drama and xianxia multi-shot work. Treat it as a continuity contract, not decorative prompt filler.

## Lock Before Shots

```text
World rule: historical court, jianghu, sect, immortal realm, or a defined hybrid.
Social relation: rank, sect, kinship, vow, debt, or forbidden attachment.
Costume rule: silhouette, layers, fabric weight, fastening, hair arrangement, footwear, and wear state.
Anchor prop: its owner, current state, and relationship meaning.
Location rule: architecture, threshold, floor material, weather, practical light, and wind/smoke/water direction.
Power rule: source, route, receiving surface, limit, and fade-out state.
```

## Prompt Rules

- Carry the same costume, prop, weather, and light-direction state across connected shots.
- Describe silk, sleeves, hair, metal, wood, stone, water, and practical light through physical response, not generic luxury wording.
- Make magic or sword energy visible as source -> path -> contact -> consequence -> decay. Do not use unexplained particles or glowing eyes.
- Express status and intimacy through distance, threshold ownership, gaze permission, stance, and prop control.

## QA

- The world and relationship boundary are clear without exposition.
- The design does not drift into cosplay, fashion posing, mixed eras, or generic fantasy clutter.
- The final shot preserves world rules while visibly changing character or relationship state.
