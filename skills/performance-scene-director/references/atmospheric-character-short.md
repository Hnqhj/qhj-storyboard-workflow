# Atmospheric Character Short

Use this for a 5–15 second single-character beauty, fashion, historical-costume, character-promo, or atmosphere clip.

## Compress the Intent

Choose one core feeling, one main body action, one gaze or facial turn, at most a few supporting micro-actions, and one stable final state. The character must travel through one continuous bodily phrase, not reset to the reference pose or loop the same action.

## Route by Feasibility

- **A — continuous take:** one spatially continuous action and one main camera behavior.
- **B — 2–3 internal shot states:** a small progression that the active generator can plausibly execute in one pass.
- **C — separate clips + edit:** incompatible angles, identity-critical close/full-body switches, exact cuts, or several independent actions.

Choose by burden; duration alone does not force a shot count.

## Body-to-Secondary-Motion Chain

Write motion in causal order:

```text
weight shift / torso turn -> shoulder and arm path -> head and gaze -> hair/fabric/jewelry lag -> settle
```

Secondary elements follow acceleration and stop slightly after the body. They do not move independently as decorative wind effects.

## Camera Contract

Choose one main camera intention—observe, approach, arc, retreat, or reveal. Keep the face and silhouette readable at the emotional or style turn. Do not stack orbit, push, crane, whip and zoom into one short clip.

## Ending

Reserve enough time for the action consequence to settle. End on a readable gaze, silhouette, object relation, or bodily state that can cut cleanly or loop intentionally. Do not require a fixed number of seconds for the opening or ending.

## Reference Lock

The reference image locks identity, clothing and required design facts; it does not freeze breathing, blink, gaze, balance shifts or material response. Review identity separately from performance quality.

Reject fixed first/last-second formulas, universal 24 fps, compulsory shot counts and ornamental motion with no bodily cause.
