# World To Asset Handoff

Use this reference after world exploration and before generating recurring characters, monsters, weapons, props, buildings, vehicles, or video clips.

## Distill The World

Extract only stable laws:

```text
world promise:
geography/climate:
social or survival rule:
craft/technology/ritual rule:
shape language:
material family:
repair/fastener logic:
color ownership:
light/atmosphere behavior:
location pressure:
```

## Derive An Asset

For each asset:

```text
role in the world:
maker/user:
purpose:
silhouette inherited from world shapes:
materials inherited from world processes:
construction/fastener logic:
wear and residue:
ergonomics or movement consequence:
color/motif ownership:
scene proof:
continuity locks:
forbidden generic drift:
```

## Handoff To Video

Keep only:

- reference identity and role locks;
- 3-6 world laws visible in the current clip;
- current location pressure;
- material/light behavior relevant to this shot;
- action affordances created by architecture, weather, terrain, props, or scale.

Do not paste research notes, world-history exposition, or all board prompts into the video prompt.
