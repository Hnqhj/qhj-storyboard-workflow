## 1. Universal Style Selection Model

For any visual request, choose style in this order:

1. Medium domain: what kind of visual object is it?
2. Viewing context: poster, video, cover, product render, UI, frame, scene, character sheet, key art, reference board?
3. Audience effect: luxury, clarity, dread, speed, sincerity, ritual, humor, awe, intimacy, authority, rebellion?
4. Constraint system: palette, texture, line, material, composition, lens, light, motion.
5. Failure risk: cheapness, drift, clutter, incoherent realism level, cultural pastiche, generic AI gloss.

Universal style block:

```text
Medium domain:
Primary aesthetic family:
Secondary influence, if needed:
Palette ownership:
Shape/line language:
Material/texture logic:
Lighting/lens or rendering logic:
Composition/hierarchy:
Motion/editing logic, if video:
Forbidden drift:
```

## 2. Medium Domains

Use these domains to prevent vague "style" thinking:

- Photography: lens, light, film stock, subject distance, tonal response, surface truth.
- Cinema: staging, lens grammar, production design, color pipeline, editing rhythm.
- Illustration: line, shape, value, abstraction level, medium behavior.
- Animation: frame economy, smear, timing, background/character separation, compositing.
- 3D/CG: topology, materials, render engine feel, stylization hierarchy, camera physics.
- Graphic design: grid, typography, hierarchy, symbols, print process.
- Fine art: composition, brush/mark, historical language, material support, gesture.
- Architecture/interior: massing, circulation, light, material junctions, scale.
- Product/industrial: silhouette, seams, ergonomics, surface finish, use marks.
- Fashion/beauty: silhouette, fabric, styling density, skin/hair treatment, editorial attitude.
- UI/data: information hierarchy, spacing, affordances, density, system language.
- Craft/material culture: handmade logic, local materials, joinery, weaving, patina, ritual use.
- Experimental/digital: algorithmic rules, projection, glitch, volumetric, scan, generative systems.

## 3. Photography Aesthetics

### Documentary Naturalism

Use for sincerity, social observation, real-world texture.

Traits:
- available light, imperfect framing, human-scale distance, honest skin and surfaces, understated color

Avoid:
- beauty lighting, staged perfection, dramatic fake grading

Prompt:

```text
documentary naturalism: available light, human-scale framing, unpolished surfaces, honest skin texture, restrained color, image feels observed rather than staged
```

### Editorial Fashion Photography

Use for luxury, character attitude, magazine covers.

Traits:
- controlled studio or location light, clean styling hierarchy, sculptural pose, deliberate negative space, fabric and skin polish

Avoid:
- random accessories, e-commerce pose, overdone glamour filter

### Street Photography

Use for chance, urban tension, social life.

Traits:
- candid composition, decisive moment, layered foreground/background, natural urban light, imperfect but meaningful geometry

Avoid:
- staged model behavior, generic street neon

### Fine-Art Minimal Photography

Use for quiet, concept, object study.

Traits:
- sparse frame, tonal subtlety, controlled shadow, object isolation, restrained palette

Avoid:
- empty frame without tension, random props

### Product Macro Photography

Use for tactile proof and premium object detail.

Traits:
- macro lens, shallow depth, crisp contact shadows, controlled specular highlights, visible surface imperfections

Avoid:
- global gloss, fake shine, sterile 3D export feel

### Film-Still Photography

Use for narrative atmosphere in a still.

Traits:
- frame feels like a moment before/after action, motivated light, background story, cinematic crop

Avoid:
- pose without story, wallpaper-like beauty

## 4. Cinema And Moving Image Aesthetics

### Natural-Light Realism

Use for poetic realism and tactile environments.

Traits:
- motivated sunlight/window/practical light, long tonal rolloff, atmosphere visible in air, understated camera

Avoid:
- overlit cinematic fake glow, random lens flare

### Noir / Neo-Noir

Use for secrecy, danger, loneliness.

Traits:
- hard shadows, black fields, practical highlights, wet reflection, partial face concealment

Avoid:
- generic blue-purple cyberpunk, muddy darkness hiding action

### Brutalist Institutional Cinema

Use for oppressive systems, bureaucracy, surveillance.

Traits:
- concrete/ceramic masses, flat overhead light, static or slow camera, symmetrical pressure, human smallness

Avoid:
- random dystopian clutter

### Operatic / Baroque Cinema

Use for sacred drama, revenge, mythic scale.

Traits:
- deep chiaroscuro, theatrical diagonals, rich fabric, dramatic shafts of light, slow reveals

Avoid:
- overusing smoke and gold

### Dream Logic Cinema

Use for uncanny emotional states.

Traits:
- soft continuity breaks, surreal but stable motif, selective impossible detail, sound/visual mismatch

Avoid:
- random surreal objects without emotional role

### Kinetic Action Cinema

Use for fights, chases, vehicles.

Traits:
- clear geography, strong screen direction, foreground parallax, speed contrast, readable impacts

Avoid:
- camera chaos, global blur, every shot at peak intensity

## 5. Illustration, Comics, And Animation

### Ligne Claire

Use for clean readability, elegant adventure, architectural clarity.

Traits:
- even clean line, flat color, precise environment, low texture, clear silhouette

Avoid:
- messy shading, over-rendered surfaces

### Graphic Novel Noir

Use for crime, moral pressure, bold action.

Traits:
- heavy black masses, high contrast, expressive panels, limited palette, sharp facial shadow

Avoid:
- gray mush, uncontrolled crosshatching

### Manga Impact

Use for speed, exaggerated emotion, action peaks.

Traits:
- dynamic panel composition, speed emphasis, expression compression, impact frames, clear silhouette

Avoid:
- random speed lines covering action, face drift, overbusy panels

### European Editorial Illustration

Use for stylish concepts, culture, magazine essays.

Traits:
- simplified shapes, witty metaphor, mature palette, textured but clean surfaces

Avoid:
- children's-book cuteness if not intended

### Children's Book Sophistication

Use for warmth, wonder, tenderness.

Traits:
- simple shapes, soft material tactility, emotional gesture, restrained color, crafted texture

Avoid:
- sticker clipart, candy saturation

### Experimental Animation

Use for abstraction, music video, conceptual motion.

Traits:
- repeated motif, procedural rhythm, shape transformation, frame-by-frame imperfection, synchronized motion design

Avoid:
- incoherent random morphing

## 6. Graphic Design And Print

### Swiss Modernism

Use for clarity, hierarchy, conceptual restraint.

Traits:
- grid, alignment, sans typography, strong scale contrast, limited palette

Avoid:
- decorative clutter, fake sophistication

### Bauhaus / Constructivist Geometry

Use for systems, energy, industrial optimism, political urgency.

Traits:
- geometric primitives, diagonals, primary/limited colors, bold asymmetry

Avoid:
- symbol overload, accidental retro cosplay

### Brutalist Graphic Design

Use for rawness, anti-polish, institutional critique.

Traits:
- exposed grid, harsh crop, utilitarian type, rough texture, asymmetric pressure

Avoid:
- unreadable ugliness without purpose

### Risograph / Zine

Use for handmade culture, music poster, youth texture.

Traits:
- spot colors, paper grain, halftone, slight misregistration, tactile imperfection

Avoid:
- muddy full-spectrum noise

### Luxury Editorial Minimalism

Use for fashion, product, gallery, architecture.

Traits:
- extreme restraint, careful spacing, one hero image, refined typography, subtle paper/material texture

Avoid:
- "premium" by adding gold and gloss everywhere

### Maximalist Editorial

Use for cultural overload, pop, youth, chaos with design control.

Traits:
- dense but gridded layers, typographic rhythm, intentional clash, repeating motif

Avoid:
- random collage without hierarchy

## 7. Fine Art And Art-Historical Families

Use art history as visual grammar, not as empty name-dropping.

### Renaissance Order

Traits:
- balanced composition, controlled perspective, idealized anatomy, calm light

Use for:
- dignity, harmony, human-centered grandeur

### Baroque Drama

Traits:
- diagonal movement, high contrast, theatrical light, emotional intensity

Use for:
- conflict, revelation, martyrdom, revenge

### Romantic Sublime

Traits:
- tiny human vs overwhelming nature, atmospheric scale, awe and danger

Use for:
- storms, mountains, cosmic or mythic landscapes

### Impressionist Light

Traits:
- broken color, atmosphere, transient light, visible brush energy

Use for:
- memory, weather, fleeting mood

### Symbolist Dream

Traits:
- allegorical objects, ritual pose, uncanny stillness, muted myth

Use for:
- psychological and mythic concepts

### Surrealist Displacement

Traits:
- ordinary objects in impossible relations, clean illusionistic rendering, dream logic

Use for:
- paradox, desire, absurdity

### Abstract Expressionist Gesture

Traits:
- large gestural marks, material force, emotional scale

Use for:
- inner violence, energy, nonliteral emotion

### Minimalist Objecthood

Traits:
- reduction, repetition, material presence, spatial relation

Use for:
- clarity, silence, ritual, conceptual seriousness

## 8. 3D, CG, Game, And Virtual Production

### Stylized 3D / NPR

Traits:
- simplified forms, physical light, controlled outlines, material-aware but not photoreal

Use for:
- characters, vehicles, fantasy scenes, readable videos

### Photoreal CG

Traits:
- physically based materials, accurate light transport, camera/lens realism, surface imperfections

Use for:
- product, architecture, VFX, grounded scenes

### Toy-Like Stylized

Traits:
- rounded forms, soft materials, miniature scale, clean colors

Use for:
- playful objects, friendly mascots, charming worlds

Avoid:
- accidental childishness in serious scenes

### Game Key Art

Traits:
- heroic readable silhouette, dramatic pose, clear factions/colors, environmental story

Use for:
- character/vehicle/world promotion

Avoid:
- armor/detail clutter that destroys silhouette

### Virtual Production Realism

Traits:
- real camera feel, LED-wall style lighting, physical set foreground, CG background depth

Use for:
- hybrid live/CG scenes

## 12. UI, Interface, And Information Aesthetics

### Enterprise Operational UI

Traits:
- dense but clear layout, restrained color, tables/cards only where functional, scan-friendly hierarchy

Use for:
- dashboards, tools, systems

### Luxury Minimal Interface

Traits:
- large spacing, few controls, subtle motion, refined typography, dark/light material depth

Use for:
- premium apps, galleries, fashion/product sites

### Diegetic Sci-Fi UI

- film/game scenes, scanning, HUD

- random floating UI overlays

- chart as argument, clear labels, restrained palette, typographic hierarchy

- reports, infographics, explainers

## 13. Material Culture, Craft, And Regional Visual Systems

Use with care: define material logic and cultural function, not superficial motifs.

### East Asian Ink And Paper Systems

Traits:
- brush pressure, negative space, seal color, paper absorbency, seasonal restraint

### Japanese Woodblock / Print Systems

Traits:
- flat color, contour, pattern, paper, stylized nature/motion

### Chinese Literati / Gongbi Contrast

Traits:
- literati: spare gesture, poetic emptiness; gongbi: precise line, mineral color, controlled detail

### Islamic Geometric / Tile Systems

Traits:
- geometry, tessellation, calligraphic rhythm, glazed tile, light and courtyard logic

### Indian Miniature / Textile Systems

Traits:
- flattened perspective, saturated but disciplined color, ornamented borders, narrative density

### African Textile / Mask / Pattern Systems

Traits:
- bold rhythm, symbolic geometry, material tactility, ceremonial function

### Latin American Mural / Folk Modern Systems

Traits:
- public scale, social narrative, strong color, simplified figures, political/material context

Avoid:
- mixing cultural symbols as decoration without function or coherence

## 14. Experimental And Contemporary Digital Aesthetics

### Glitch

Use for:
- corrupted memory, system failure, media breakdown

Rules:
- glitch must affect a specific channel, surface, or signal; avoid random full-frame chaos

### Generative / Algorithmic

Use for:
- systems, emergence, procedural worlds

Rules:
- define repetition, parameter, variation, and constraint

### Scan / Point Cloud

Use for:
- memory capture, surveillance, reconstruction

Rules:
- points should reveal depth and missing data, not become glitter

### Projection Mapping

Use for:
- architecture/event/ritual overlays

Rules:
- projection must follow surface geometry and light falloff

### Volumetric Light / Holographic

Use for:
- future interfaces, ghosts, spatial data

Rules:
- define emitter, opacity, occlusion, and environmental interaction

## 15. Mood-To-Style Routing

- Elegant: editorial minimalism, couture editorial, fine-art minimal photography
- Brutal: brutalist architecture/design, woodcut, noir, rugged functional
- Fast: kinetic cinema, manga impact, vector motion, foreground parallax
- Tender: children's-book sophistication, natural-light photography, soft craft textures
- Sacred: ritual space, baroque light, icon-like symmetry, ink restraint
- Alien: avant-garde fashion, organic architecture, experimental CG
- Institutional: Swiss grids, brutalist space, overexposed surveillance light
- Rebellious: constructivist diagonals, streetwear editorial, zine/risograph
- Dreamlike: symbolist, dream cinema, faded print memory, cut-paper parallax
- Luxurious: editorial minimalism, baroque chiaroscuro, luxury craft object
- Scientific: product macro, data visualization, minimal industrial, lab white/steel
- Nostalgic: film-still photography, aged print, analog interface, hand-worn materials
- Mythic: romantic sublime, ritual space, ink wash, ancient material evidence

## 16. Universal Prompt Assembly

Use this when packaging:

```text
Art direction:
This image/video belongs to [medium domain], using [primary aesthetic family] with [optional secondary influence]. The style is defined by [palette ownership], [shape/line language], [material/texture behavior], [lighting/lens/rendering logic], and [composition hierarchy]. [Motion/editing rule if video]. Avoid [specific drift/failure modes].
```

Example:

```text
Art direction:
This 15-second action video belongs to graphic animation, using woodcut noir with controlled manga-impact timing. The world is black/white/gray paper and ink; only the protagonist's weapon trail owns vermilion red. Shapes are carved silhouettes and hard diagonal cuts; texture is dry paper grain and rough ink edges; lighting is graphic side contrast, not realistic neon. Impact frames may invert red/black for less than 0.15 seconds, then return to the same world. Avoid style drift, random particles, decorative red lines, and live-action realism.
```
