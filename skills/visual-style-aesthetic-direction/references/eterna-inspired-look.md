# ETERNA-Inspired Look Capsule

Use this as an optional look, not a universal cinema default and not a claim of official Fujifilm reproduction.

## Matching levels

```text
inspired: prompt-level tendency only
reference-grade: compared against an approved still/reference, still not color-science replication
color-managed: known input space/log curve + matching transform or authorized LUT
+ declared display/output target + measured and visual validation
```

Do not call a normal generated image color-managed because the prompt says Rec.709, sRGB, F-Log or LUT. A camera Log-to-display LUT is valid only for its matching input encoding or a deliberately transformed equivalent.

## Visible look controls

- restrained saturation and accent count;
- soft highlight roll-off without chalky clipping;
- readable, colored shadow structure rather than crushed neutral black;
- protected natural skin relationship;
- muted olive/sage greens and steel blue-gray only when the scene supports them;
- limited orange/teal separation, HDR microcontrast and glossy neon;
- motivated practical or natural light, calm large value masses and controlled negative space.

Express these as relative relationships. Fixed HEX palettes and universal saturation percentages are creative anchors, not official values or pass/fail thresholds.

## Prompt block

```text
ETERNA-inspired restrained cinema palette: muted saturation, soft protected highlights,
readable cool-gray shadow structure, natural skin relationships, limited accent colors,
calm large value masses, no aggressive orange-teal split, no HDR microcontrast or neon gloss.
```

## Boundary

- If the user needs a real grade, inspect the source profile/log curve and route to a color-managed toolchain.
- Do not promise recoverable dynamic range from a flattened 8-bit image.
- Lens/focal-length words remain visual intent, not physical proof.
- Keep this capsule subordinate to story, subject identity and scene lighting.
