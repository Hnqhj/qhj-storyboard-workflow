# Aesthetic Quality Gates

Use this to diagnose why an image or video feels cheap, generic, or not高级.

## Advanced Stylization

高级风格化 is not "more style." It is a controlled system.

It usually has:
- one dominant visual idea
- strong hierarchy and readable focal point
- restrained palette with color ownership
- medium-specific texture, not random noise
- motivated lighting
- meaningful negative space
- consistent shape language
- effects that have cause and consequence
- a final frame that works as graphic design, not only as a screenshot

## Cheap Stylization Symptoms

### Name Salad

Symptom:
- prompt lists many famous styles, mediums, and quality words.

Fix:
- choose one primary style family and translate it into medium, line, color, light, texture, and motion.

### Effect-First Thinking

Symptom:
- particles, glow, speed lines, brush strokes, or glitch effects carry the image.

Fix:
- make composition, silhouette, and action readable before effects.

### No Color Ownership

Symptom:
- colors drift by shot or all colors appear everywhere.

Fix:
- assign colors by meaning: protagonist, antagonist, world, danger, memory, authority, magic, technology.

### Texture As Dirt

Symptom:
- paper grain, scratches, dust, ink, or grunge appears everywhere equally.

Fix:
- put texture where the medium needs it; keep focal areas clean enough to read.

### Incoherent Realism Level

Symptom:
- face looks real, body looks cartoon, background looks game CG, effects look sticker-like.

Fix:
- define a rendering hierarchy: character style, environment style, effects style, and how light connects them.

### Decorative Symbolism

Symptom:
- lines, shapes, UI, sigils, or symbols appear because they look cool.

Fix:
- each symbol must show power, route, ownership, warning, memory, injury, ritual, or function.

### Flat Poster Without Design

Symptom:
- image is flat because there is no depth plan, not because it is intentionally graphic.

Fix:
- define foreground, midground, background, and focal hierarchy even in flat styles.

## The 7-Point Aesthetic Check

1. Focal Point: Can the eye find the subject in one second?
2. Hierarchy: Is there clear primary, secondary, and tertiary information?
3. Constraint: Are palette, shapes, and textures limited?
4. Medium: Does the image behave like its chosen medium?
5. Motive: Does each effect or motif have a reason?
6. Contrast: Is there contrast of scale, value, texture, speed, or silence?
7. End Image: Would the final frame work as a poster or key art?

## Prompt Patch Patterns

### Make It More高级

```text
Reduce decorative effects. Use one dominant visual system: [style family]. Limit the palette to [colors and owners]. Keep one strong focal point. Texture appears only as [medium-specific behavior]. Use negative space and clean silhouette. Every accent must have a story or motion function.
```

### Fix Cheap Cartooning

```text
Preserve stylized shapes, but add disciplined lighting and material response: clear planes, controlled rim light, contact shadows, fabric folds, surface roughness, and stable linework. Avoid chibi proportions, plastic gloss, random oversaturation, and simplified toy-like anatomy.
```

### Fix Generic AI Gloss

```text
Remove global shine and over-clean surfaces. Add selective roughness, matte/gloss contrast, localized wear, paper/ink/fabric topology, and directional light that reveals real surface behavior. Keep the style graphic, not photoreal.
```

### Fix Overdesigned Style

```text
Remove secondary motifs. Keep only [main motif]. Simplify background shapes. Use 2-3 value masses. Let the strongest silhouette and one accent color carry the frame.
```
