# Supernatural VFX Language Atlas

Use this file to prevent all supernatural effects from collapsing into colored glow, generic particles, circular magic rings, and beam attacks. Select one governing grammar, then adapt it to the story, culture, medium, and power level.

## Universal Construction

Before appearance, define:

```text
power source -> activation protocol -> formation law -> spatial behavior ->
target interaction -> resistance/cost -> state change -> decay/residue
```

The effect's identity must remain readable through shape, motion, material analogy, timing, and environmental response even when color is removed.

## 1. General Magic

Best for invented fantasy systems.

- Source: body, staff, relic, familiar, terrain, celestial body, stored charge.
- Activation: gesture, spoken command, contact, drawing, sacrifice, emotional threshold.
- Shape grammar: runic geometry, crystalline facets, fluid ribbons, smoke organisms, woven threads, spatial folds.
- Motion grammar: assemble, orbit, braid, inhale, discharge, bloom, collapse.
- Result: projectile, field, binding, transformation, displacement, material conversion.

Avoid defining a school only by color. Give every school a distinct source, shape family, motion verb, collision behavior, and residue.

## 2. 阴阳术 / 阴阳道-Inspired Effects

Core read: **ritual calculation and enforced order**, not free-form energy emission.

- Cultural basis: yin-yang opposition and transformation; five-phase correspondences; Japanese Onmyodo historically combined Chinese yin-yang/five-phase ideas with local ritual beliefs.
- Activation media: talisman paper, seal, fan, bell, mirror, comb, blade, cord, vessel, written mark, hand sign, summoned intermediary.
- Spatial grammar: directional gates, layered planes, cardinal or five-direction correspondence, alternating yin/yang rotation, measured nodes, barriers, binding lines, sealed thresholds.
- Motion grammar: write, unfold, pin, turn, exchange, invert, bind, command, close.
- Material analogy: ink soaking paper, ash, braided cord, engraved metal, lacquer, candle flame, folded paper, brushed calligraphy.
- Collision/result: repel, redirect, expose, immobilize, purify, summon, substitute, seal.
- Residue: burnt or blank talisman, broken cord, extinguished node, ash-written trace, cooling engraved line.

A ritual array should have an anchor, orientation, target rule, operating sequence, and closure. It should not behave like a generic luminous floor decal.

## 3. 修仙 / 仙侠 Effects

Core read: **cultivation, law, artifact, and heaven-earth scale**.

- Source hierarchy: body meridians/true essence -> weapon or artifact -> formation/domain -> dharma image or celestial phenomenon.
- Activation: breath gathering, mudra or sword formula, artifact awakening, qi circulation, sacrifice, law invocation.
- Shape grammar: sword intent as compressed directional planes; talisman and formation as operating structures; dharma image as a large-scale will silhouette; domain as altered environmental law.
- Motion grammar: gather, circulate, condense, divide, manifest, suppress, descend, annihilate, return.
- Scale progression: local body cue -> weapon response -> surrounding matter response -> terrain/sky phenomenon. Do not begin every attack at maximum scale.
- Result: suppression, severing, soul restraint, elemental transformation, spatial sealing, formation control, artifact contest.
- Residue: cooling sword mark, exhausted talisman, broken formation nodes, dispersed spiritual mist, altered terrain, dimmed celestial image.

The environment must participate: cloud layers, water, dust, vegetation, architecture, and light should reveal the governing force. Large effects need slower secondary motion and clear reference objects to prove scale.

## 4. Elemental and Five-Phase Systems

Do not equate an element with a stock particle preset.

- Metal: convergence, cutting planes, resonance, reflection, rigid ordering.
- Wood: branching, growth fronts, tension, rooting, parasitic spread.
- Water: pressure, flow path, reflection, phase change, engulfing volume.
- Fire: fuel, temperature, convection, consumption, ember decay.
- Earth: mass, compression, fracture, uplift, settling.

When using five-phase logic, express generation or restraint through a visible operation and result, not merely through five colors.

## 5. Summons, 法相, and Spirit Manifestations

- Establish host, medium, or contract anchor.
- Show formation from a coherent frontier or assembled components.
- Give the summoned entity weight, occlusion, parallax, and interaction with atmosphere/light.
- Separate proxy action from host action while preserving their causal link.
- A 法相 should magnify intent and governing law, not function as an unrelated giant mascot.
- End through recall, collapse, sealing, fracture, or dissolution that matches its formation material.

## 6. Prompt Compilation Rule

For every supernatural hero beat, include only the visible essentials:

```text
named system identity + source/anchor + activation + dominant shape +
spatial path/field behavior + target change + environment response + decay
```

Do not list every possible layer. One dominant grammar plus one supporting material analogy is stronger than a collage of circles, runes, smoke, lightning, petals, particles, beams, and shockwaves.
