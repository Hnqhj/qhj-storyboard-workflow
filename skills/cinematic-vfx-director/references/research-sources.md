# Primary Research Sources

These sources support the mechanisms in this skill. Use the transferable principle, not a decorative name-drop.

## Stylized motion and animation impact

- Sony Pictures Imageworks, *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*: artist intention, animation on twos, geometry smears, speed lines, multiple limbs, 2D effects over 3D.  
  https://www.imageworks.com/our-craft/feature-animation/movies/spider-man-spider-verse
- Mariel Cartwright, GDC, *Powerful and Effective Animation*: silhouette, anticipation, smears, overshoot, selective hitstop, key-pose contrast.  
  https://media.gdcvault.com/gdcchina14/presentations/833784_MarielCartwright_PowerfulAndEffective_EN.pdf
- Arc System Works, *Guilty Gear Xrd's Art Style*: artist-controlled keys, limited interpolation, expressive deformation, dynamic special-move cameras.  
  https://www.ggxrd.com/Motomura_Junya_GuiltyGearXrd.pdf
- Disney Research, *Programmable Motion Effects*: controllable speed lines, stroboscopic multiples, temporal offsets, and blur families.  
  https://la.disneyresearch.com/publication/programmable-motion-effects/

## Readability, ownership, timing, and threat

- Riot Games Art Education, Visual Effects: VFX fundamentals and clarity.  
  https://www.riotgames.com/vi/artedu/visual-effects
- Riot Games portfolio guidance: shape, value, color, timing, thematic cohesion, attribution to source.  
  https://www.riotgames.com/en/portfolio-and-reel-suggestions
- GDC, *VFX as a Game Design Language*: focal point, identity, area, threat, anticipation cues.  
  https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1027899/Visual-Effects-Summit-VFX-as

## Art-directed simulation and stylization

- Disney Animation, *Art Directing Effects for Tangled*: retimed and sculpted simulations, layered foam/spray, scale through motion.  
  https://media.disneyanimation.com/uploads/production/publication_asset/50/asset/tangled-water.pdf
- Disney Animation, *Myth: A Frozen Tale — Stylized Effects*: directed vector fields, 2D loops in 3D, focus masks, environment-specific debris.  
  https://media.disneyanimation.com/technology/publications/2020/siggraph_2020_MYTH_FX_talk.pdf
- Disney Animation, *Moana — Foundation of a Lava Monster*: modular rigs, proxy/blocking, directable wind, layered simulations and render controls.  
  https://media.disneyanimation.com/uploads/production/publication_asset/166/asset/Moana_Foundation_of_a_Lava_Monster.pdf
- Pixar RenderMan, *Stylization at Pixar*: stylization across shape, camera, material, light and simulation; art-directed shadows and performance-led volumes.  
  https://renderman.pixar.com/stories/stylization-at-pixar

## Simulation, events, collision, destruction, and compositing

- SideFX Houdini Pyro and destruction documentation: source fields, bulk motion before noise, cooling/dissipation, material fracture, constraints, impact-sourced debris.  
  https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/pyro/pyro.html  
  https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/destruction/overview.html  
  https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/destruction/constraints.html
- Epic Games Niagara documentation: modular systems, emitters, events, collision, secondary behavior, particle lights.  
  https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/overview-of-niagara-effects-for-unreal-engine  
  https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/events-and-event-handlers-in-niagara-effects-for-unreal-engine
- Foundry Nuke documentation: deep compositing, holdouts, light wrap and grain matching.  
  https://learn.foundry.com/nuke/16.0/content/comp_environment/deep/deep_compositing.html  
  https://learn.foundry.com/nuke/content/reference_guide/draw_nodes/lightwrap.html
- Blender documentation: scene-linear color management and vector/motion blur behavior.  
  https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/color_management.html  
  https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.0/compositing/types/filter/blur/vector_blur.html

## Supernatural systems, magic layering, and cultural grammar

- Unity, *Create spellbinding visual effects with our advanced VFX guide*: event-driven effects, timeline control, linked systems, shader/VFX integration, vector fields, point caches, and layered sample effects.  
  https://unity.com/blog/games/create-spellbinding-visual-effects-with-advanced-vfx-guide
- SideFX Houdini 22, Particle FX: magic trails assembled from layered particle components, spiral paths, star-shaped sources, dust, and sparse smoke.  
  https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/news/22/mpm.html
- GDC, *VFX as a Game Design Language*: VFX communicates focal point, identity, area, threat, and gameplay state; spectacle must preserve readability.  
  https://media.gdcvault.com/GDC%2B2022/Speaker%2BSlides/VFXasagamedesignlanguage_Nguyen_An-Tim.pdf
- 原力数字科技《凡人修仙传》制作组：打戏、阵法、法相和星宫开海 SFX 制作讲解，提供仙侠效果的阵法、法相、环境尺度与动作结合案例。  
  https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1qu411K7Dw/
- 故宫博物院，阴阳五行：阴阳消长、五行及其相生相克与多重对应体系。  
  https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/241541.html
- 日本国家文化财机构 e国宝《驱邪绘·钟馗》：阴阳道结合中国阴阳五行思想与日本固有咒术信仰的历史说明，以及驱邪神图像资料。  
  https://emuseum.nich.go.jp/detail?content_base_id=100247&content_part_id=5&langId=zh
