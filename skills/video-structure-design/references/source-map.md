# Source Map

Research sources used to shape this skill. Use these as anchors, not as copied templates.

## Platform And Commercial Video Structure

- Google Ads Help, `About the ABCDs of effective video ads`: used for the Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction checkpoint model for video ads.
  - https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en
- Google Business / YouTube Ads, `ABCDs of effective video ads`: used for video ad structure and creative best-practice checkpoints.
  - https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/
- Think with Google, `ABCDs PDF Playbook`: used for the broader ABCD ad framework and objective-based creative thinking.
  - https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/15987/ABCDs_PDFPlaybook_April2022_Final.pdf
- TikTok For Business Blog, `Creative Best Practices for TikTok Ads`: used for short-form creative principles including fast hook, native style, and structured engagement.
  - https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads
- Meta for Business, `Instagram & Facebook Reels Ads`: used as a platform reference for Reels ad placement and short-form ad context.
  - https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads

## Video Script And Production Structure

- Wistia, `How to Write a Video Script`: used for script planning, audience focus, and production-oriented video scripting.
  - https://wistia.com/learn/production/wistias-scripting-tips
- Wistia, `Video how-tos`: used as a production-learning reference for writing, shooting, and structuring videos.
  - https://wistia.com/watch/video-how-tos

## Existing Local Skills To Combine

- `screenwriting-story-craft`: use after this skill when structure needs character, conflict, scene, and dialogue.
- `cinematic-audiovisual-language`: use after this skill when structure needs shot function, screen direction, staging, and editing grammar.
- `high-tension-shot-design`: use after structure and audiovisual grammar are sound, to intensify composition and movement.
