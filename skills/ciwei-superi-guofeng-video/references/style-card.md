# Style Card

Use this reference for visual taste, identity adaptation, scene/material choices, and negative prompt vocabulary.

## Visual North Star

The image should feel like a key story frame from a Chinese historical drama:

- real person, real set, real fabric, real light
- restrained, elegant, emotional, not loud
- refined but not overdesigned
- cinematic but not glossy
- ancient Chinese atmosphere without cheap studio/cosplay feeling

Avoid:

- internet-celebrity face, plastic doll face, over-smoothed skin
- stock costume portrait, studio photoshoot, character turntable
- high-saturation fantasy filters, game poster, anime, oil-painting look
- chaotic embroidery, melted fabric, unstable ornaments, fake background

## Face Style

Use natural East Asian facial features:

- clean realistic skin with fine texture
- refined oval or naturally small face, but not exaggerated influencer face
- clear expressive eyes, not empty
- restrained classical makeup
- natural nose, lips, jaw relationship
- soft facial contour with believable bone structure

Do not use oily face, glass skin, waxy skin, over-bright lips, heavy makeup, excessive beautification, or copied celebrity identity.

## Emotional Palette

Common emotions:

- 克制
- 隐忍
- 警觉
- 失落
- 不甘
- 决绝
- 温柔但有心事
- 冷淡疏离
- 心虚回避
- 压抑的情绪刚露出又被压回去

The emotion should be visible through eyes, brow, lips, breath, posture, fingers, or a tiny pause. Do not make the character theatrically exaggerated.

## Lighting

Prefer:

- warm candlelight
- lantern light
- soft window light
- side/back rim light
- restrained golden palace light
- low-saturation warm interior light
- shallow-depth background bokeh

The light must serve emotion. Avoid overexposure, plastic shine, strong synthetic specular highlights, and flat beauty lighting.

## Color

Useful directions:

- black and gold: power, palace pressure, cold elegance
- red and gold: conflict, status, emotional heat
- celadon/green-blue: cool, dreamlike, tender distance
- violet/mauve: noble restraint, quiet emotion
- warm wood/brown: believable interior and human warmth
- white/light blue: clean, fragile, scholarly, or restrained

Keep the palette controlled. Do not make the result dirty, oversaturated, or one-note.

## Composition

Prefer:

- vertical 9:16
- half-body close shot
- bust close-up
- eye-focused close-up
- full-body medium palace shot when costume matters
- foreground veil, bead curtain, window lattice, lantern, or sleeve for depth
- shallow depth of field with face and hands clear

Keep attention on eyes, face, hands, costume texture, and the story beat.

## Identity Adaptation

Do not force every identity into palace clothing. Match costume, scene, prop, and social world to the role.

宫廷贵女 / 妃子 / 公主:

- palace interior, corridor, screen, candle, bead curtain
- silk robe, embroidered palace dress, gold thread, hairpin, phoenix ornament
- restrained power, hidden rivalry, emotional pressure

宫女 / 侍女 / 女官:

- side hall, corridor, curtain, tray, scroll, robe sleeve
- clean but lower-status costume, practical hair ornament
- caution, suppressed emotion, waiting, overheard secret

平民少女 / 农家女:

- courtyard, wooden house, village lane, field edge, morning light, rain-after light
- clean cloth garments, simple hair tie, basket, water basin, flowers
- natural, quiet, grounded, not dirty or cheap

渔女 / 船娘:

- riverside, boat cabin, wet wood, mist, wind, water reflection
- plain layered cloth, damp fabric edge, hair moved by wind
- longing, alertness, farewell, waiting

卖花女 / 市井人物:

- market street, flower basket, tea stall, lantern street, alley
- simple but clean costume, everyday props
- human warmth, tension under ordinary life

茶馆老板娘 / 酒肆女子:

- tea house, wooden table, steam, warm window light, lantern, curtain
- practical but graceful costume, hairpin, cup, tray, ledger
- worldly, observant, calm under pressure

绣娘 / 医女 / 画师 / 手艺人:

- embroidery room, medicine cabinet, study, window light, fabric, herbs, ink, scroll
- work-focused props, refined hands, quiet concentration
- delicate tension, hidden thought, private decision

书生 / 女先生 / 教书先生:

- study, books, scrolls, ink, window lattice, paper
- restrained robe, clean hair, scholarly air
- hesitation, resolve, quiet sorrow

江湖侠女 / 女将 / 刺客 / 镖师:

- inn, bamboo forest, snow street, long corridor, rain, gate, horse station
- practical layered clothing, sword, cloak, hat, leather/fabric mix
- danger, restraint, alertness, suppressed motion

皇帝 / 权臣 / 贵公子:

- palace hall, study, long corridor, screen, candlelight
- dark robe, gold embroidery, jade belt, official hairpiece
- power, pressure, calculation, controlled emotion

## Negative Prompt Vocabulary

Use a compact subset, not every term every time:

油腻的脸，塑料脸，假脸，蜡像感，玻璃皮，过度磨皮，网红脸，五官变形，脸部融化，眼神空洞，表情僵硬，嘴唇过油，廉价古装写真，影楼风，cosplay感，服装塑料感，纹样混乱，头饰变形，发簪融合，流苏乱飞，布料融化，背景杂乱，AI感过强，过度锐化，高饱和滤镜，二次元，游戏立绘，文字，水印，logo，手指畸形，多手指。
