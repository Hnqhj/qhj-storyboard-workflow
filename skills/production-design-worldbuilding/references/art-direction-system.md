# Art Direction System

Use this for palette, shape, material, motif, and lighting rules.

## Color Ownership

Assign colors by meaning:

```text
World: ivory ceramic, warm gold, pale coral.
Character: matte black, chrome silver, electric cyan.
Authority: sterile white and red warning panels.
Danger: overexposed gold light and erased shadows.
Memory: faded gray printed codes.
```

This prevents random palette drift.

## Shape Language

Choose 2-3 dominant shape rules:

- vertical slabs: institutional pressure, hierarchy, city scale
- horizontal bands: speed, transit, control lanes
- circular gates: ritual, scanning, border, repetition
- blade diagonals: rebellion, intrusion, danger
- soft fabric folds: humanity against hard systems
- nested mechanical segments: transformation, precision, engineered power

## Material Families

Group materials:

- authority world: ceramic, glass, brushed aluminum, clean rubber, luminous panels
- rebel character: matte nylon, carbon fiber, scratched chrome, black leather, analog tags
- memory/old layer: paper residue, rusted staples, faded ink, worn concrete

Do not make every surface glossy. Contrast matte, rough, translucent, reflective, worn, and absorbing areas.

## Motifs

Motifs can repeat across frames:

- barcode scars
- transit arrows
- erased faces on public screens
- ceramic ribs
- red inspection seals
- cyan illegal light trails
- analog metal ID plates

Use motifs sparingly. One or two motifs repeated are stronger than twenty symbols.

## Lighting Logic

Lighting should come from the world:

- surveillance world: flat overhead exposure, few shadows
- luxury future: soft large-source light, controlled reflections
- chase/action: hard side streaks, passing occlusion, intermittent light bands
- emotional reveal: backlight/rim light that separates subject from system

## Art Direction Prompt Block

```text
Art direction: [world colors] belong to the environment, [character colors] belong only to the protagonist, architecture follows [shape rules], props repeat [motifs], materials follow [families], lighting comes from [world source]. Avoid [forbidden drift].
```
