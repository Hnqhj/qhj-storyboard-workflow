# Short-Form Video Story

Use this for 5s, 8s, 15s, 30s, trailers, teasers, AI video, and social short-form scenes.

## Principle

Short video is not a tiny feature film. It needs one visible dramatic turn.

Good micro-story:

```text
striking situation -> immediate desire -> visible resistance -> reversal/choice -> memorable final image
```

## 5-Second Structure

```text
0-1s hook image
1-3s action or conflict
3-5s reversal, reveal, or final image
```

Use for one clean action, reveal, or transformation.

## 8-Second Structure

```text
0-2s hook and world
2-5s pursuit or pressure
5-7s turn
7-8s image button
```

Use for a single scene with one turn.

## 15-Second Structure

```text
0-3s hook: world, character, or anomaly
3-6s desire/action: protagonist moves toward something
6-9s obstacle: the world pushes back
9-12s choice/reversal: protagonist changes tactic or pays cost
12-15s consequence/end image: new status, reveal, or exit
```

Use for action entrance, character reveal, chase beat, transformation, or emotional micro-scene.

## 30-Second Structure

```text
0-5s hook and premise
5-10s character desire
10-17s complication
17-23s reversal or cost
23-28s final choice
28-30s end button
```

Use for short ads, teasers, character trailers, or micro-drama.

## AI Video Story Rules

- One protagonist.
- One visible goal.
- One main obstacle.
- One turn.
- One memorable final image.
- Keep lore as background texture, not explanation.
- Let props, costume, route, body behavior, and environment show story.

## Convert Story To Shots

After writing story beats, hand them to `cinematic-audiovisual-language`:

```text
story beat -> shot function -> shot size/angle -> movement -> cut reason
```

Example:

```text
Beat: she chooses to cross the forbidden district.
Shot function: commitment.
Camera: low rear tracking as the bike crosses the red boundary line.
Cut reason: wheel crosses line, sound shifts.
```
