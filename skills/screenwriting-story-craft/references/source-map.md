# Source Map

Research sources used to shape this skill. Use these as anchors, not as copied formulas.

## Screenwriting And Story Structure

- StudioBinder, `Three Act Structure`: used for setup, confrontation, resolution, turning points, midpoint, climax, and the practical role of act structure.
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/three-act-structure/
- StudioBinder, `How to Write a Logline`: used for logline components such as protagonist, goal, antagonist/opposition, and stakes.
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/what-is-a-logline-definition-examples/
- MasterClass, `How to Write a Treatment`: used for treatment as a prose summary of main action and story arc.
  - https://www.masterclass.com/articles/how-to-write-a-treatment

## Script Format And Professional Practice

- BBC Writersroom, script library and opportunities: used as a professional reference point for reading produced scripts and understanding script craft in practice.
  - https://www.bbc.co.uk/writersroom/scripts/
  - https://www.bbc.co.uk/writersroom/opportunities/
- Academy Nicholl Fellowships, screenplay rules/guidelines: used as a professional screenplay submission reference.
  - https://www.oscars.org/nicholl/about

## Courses And Writing Craft

- New York Film Academy, screenwriting program overview: used for the broad curriculum areas of screenwriting, structure, character, and workshop revision.
  - https://www.nyfa.edu/screenwriting/
- 中国大学MOOC / 编剧相关课程 search results: used to cross-check Chinese-language course emphasis on story, character, conflict, scene, and dialogue.
  - https://www.icourse163.org/

## AI Video Adaptation Notes

This skill adapts conventional screenwriting into short-form AI-video workflows. For AI video, prioritize filmable beats: visible desire, visible obstacle, visible choice, visible consequence, and a final image that can be handed to shot planning.
