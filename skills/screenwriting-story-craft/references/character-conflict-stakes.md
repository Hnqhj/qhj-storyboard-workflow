# Character, Conflict, And Stakes

Use this for character arcs, motives, emotional hooks, antagonists, and stakes.

## Character Engine

Define:

```text
Want: what the character actively pursues.
Need: what the character must understand, accept, or become.
Flaw/Wound: the pattern that makes the want dangerous or incomplete.
Mask: how the character performs control.
Pressure: what forces the mask to crack.
Choice: the action that proves change or refusal.
```

For AI video, make each part visible:

- Want becomes a route, object, target, escape, rescue, chase, or refusal.
- Need becomes hesitation, sacrifice, restraint, reveal, or changed behavior.
- Flaw becomes overconfidence, avoidance, obsession, fear, isolation, vanity, control.

## Conflict Types

- Physical: pursuit, obstacle, injury, locked door, countdown, dangerous terrain.
- Social: status, authority, betrayal, public shame, forbidden relationship.
- Emotional: fear, grief, desire, shame, guilt, denial.
- Moral: save one vs many, obey vs rebel, reveal truth vs protect someone.
- Ideological: freedom vs order, tradition vs future, memory vs progress.
- Environmental: city system, weather, machine, battlefield, collapsing space.

Strong scenes usually combine one external conflict with one internal conflict.

## Stakes

Stakes are not automatically bigger when more people are threatened. They become stronger when the viewer knows what loss means.

Specify:

- personal stake: what this costs the protagonist
- relational stake: who gets hurt or abandoned
- social stake: status, law, exile, public identity
- physical stake: injury, death, capture, destruction
- moral stake: who the protagonist becomes if they win

## Antagonist Force

Antagonist does not have to be a villain. It can be:

- person
- institution
- machine
- environment
- memory
- body
- desire
- time limit
- rule system

Make it specific enough to generate scenes:

```text
weak: society oppresses her
strong: the city toll gates erase her identity tag each time she crosses a district
```

## Arc Shapes

- Positive arc: false belief -> pressure -> choice -> new behavior.
- Negative arc: flaw -> success through flaw -> deeper cost -> self-destruction.
- Flat arc: character holds a truth while the world changes around them.
- Reveal arc: character seems one way, then action reveals hidden truth.

## Character Test

Would the same plot work with any other character? If yes, customize the goal, method, flaw, and final choice.
