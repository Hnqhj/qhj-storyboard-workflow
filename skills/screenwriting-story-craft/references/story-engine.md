# Story Engine

Use this for loglines, premise, central conflict, structure, and story diagnosis.

## Dramatic Engine Formula

```text
Someone wants [concrete goal], but [specific opposition] blocks them, so they [active strategy], which forces [choice/cost/reveal].
```

If any slot is vague, the story will feel soft.

## Core Terms

- Premise: the story situation and its promise.
- Logline: one compact sentence containing protagonist, goal, obstacle, stakes, and hook.
- Central dramatic question: the question the story makes the viewer wait to answer.
- Theme: the pressure underneath the plot; what the story tests or argues through action.
- Inciting incident: the disruption that makes normal life impossible.
- Turning point: a choice or event that changes the direction of the story.
- Climax: the moment when the central conflict forces its decisive choice.
- Resolution: the new condition after the choice.

## Story Structure Options

Three-act spine:

```text
Act 1: setup, protagonist, normal world, disruption, commitment.
Act 2: pursuit, complications, reversals, midpoint pressure, cost.
Act 3: final choice, climax, consequence, new state.
```

Eight-sequence style:

```text
1. world and desire
2. disruption and commitment
3. first strategy
4. complication and midpoint shift
5. consequences
6. pressure tightens
7. crisis choice
8. climax and resolution
```

Short-form spine:

```text
hook image -> desire -> obstacle -> reversal -> choice -> ending image
```

## Story Diagnosis

Ask:

- Who changes?
- What do they want right now?
- What blocks them physically, socially, emotionally, or morally?
- What do they do because of that block?
- What do they lose, reveal, or become?
- What is the final image that proves the story changed?

## Logline Patterns

Action/sci-fi:

```text
When [disruption] hits [world], [protagonist with flaw] must [goal] before [stakes], but [opposing force] turns the mission into [moral or emotional cost].
```

Character drama:

```text
A [specific person] chasing [external want] is forced by [conflict] to confront [inner need], risking [personal cost].
```

Short AI video:

```text
In [striking world], [visual protagonist] tries to [visible action], but [visual obstacle] forces [surprising choice], ending with [memorable image].
```

## Common Weaknesses

- Premise has style but no pressure.
- Backstory explains behavior instead of current action revealing it.
- The antagonist force is abstract, so scenes lack friction.
- The ending is a pose, not a consequence.
- The theme is stated in dialogue instead of tested by a choice.
