# AI Music Prompting

Check current official platform documentation before relying on exact controls.

## Prompt Stack

Use:

```text
purpose + duration/form
musical identity
tempo/meter/rhythm
harmony/mode
motif/melody
instrumentation/timbre
performance/vocal treatment
dynamics and production
emotional progression
exclusions
```

## Structure

Name sections only when useful:

```text
intro -> verse/build -> pre-drop -> drop/impact -> breakdown -> final button
```

For score cues:

```text
motif seed -> restrained development -> harmonic turn -> impact -> unresolved tail
```

## Prompt Example

```text
15-second cinematic action cue for a futuristic motorcycle reveal.
132 BPM, 4/4, tight syncopated electronic pulse with a three-note rising motif.
Low processed cello and granular engine-like synth share the same rhythmic cell.
Begin narrow and dry, add metallic percussion and octave expansion at 6 seconds,
brief half-beat silence before the 11-second impact, then an unresolved filtered tail.
No heroic brass fanfare, no generic trailer choir, no cheerful EDM drop, no vocals.
```

## Lyrics And Vocals

Specify:

- language;
- point of view;
- vocal character;
- syllable density;
- rhyme or free verse;
- section role;
- spoken, sung, whispered, choral, or instrumental;
- words or themes to avoid.

Do not overload short-form score prompts with lyrics unless the vocal is the point.

## Exclusions

Use exclusions to remove concrete drift:

- no vocals;
- no choir;
- no acoustic drums;
- no bright major-key resolution;
- no generic trailer riser;
- no long ambient intro;
- no abrupt genre switch;
- no excessive mastering loudness.

## Platform Notes

- Suno supports style descriptions, custom lyrics/instrumental choices, exclusions, and creative controls that may change over time.
- Udio recommends descriptive prompts and guidance tags for structure/performance; exact features may change.

Verify current controls from official help pages before giving platform-specific instructions.
