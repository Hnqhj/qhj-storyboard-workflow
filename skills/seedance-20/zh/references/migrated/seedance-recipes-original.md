# seedance-recipes · 题材模板 (v5.0)

本 skill 为 Seedance 2.0 最常见的七大内容类别提供可直接用于生产的提示词模板。各题材的完整拆解参见 [ref:genre-guides]。

---

## 1. 产品 / 电商

**目标：** 干净、聚焦、高保真的产品展示。

```
// 360° Orbit Template
A [product] on a [surface]. Smooth 360-degree orbit camera. Soft studio key light, rim highlight. Macro depth-of-field. 4K pristine detail. No text.
```

**示例：** `A glass perfume bottle on a white marble surface. Smooth 360-degree orbit camera. Soft studio key light, rim highlight. Macro depth-of-field. 4K pristine detail. No text.`

---

## 2. 生活方式 / 社交媒体

**目标：** 适用于 TikTok、Instagram 等平台的、有共鸣的、真实感十足的瞬间。

```
// Morning Routine Template
A [person] [action] in a [location]. Handheld phone perspective, slight sway. They [emotion]. [Aesthetic].
```

**示例：** `A young woman makes coffee in a sunlit kitchen. Handheld phone perspective, slight sway. She smiles as she sips the coffee. Warm, cozy aesthetic.`

---

## 3. 剧情 / 叙事

**目标：** 具有一致角色和情感分量的电影化叙事。

```
// Suspense Corridor Template
@Image1 character reference. A [character] cautiously [action] down a [scene]. They [action]. The camera follows closely behind them, handheld. The only sound is [sound].
```

**示例：** `@Image1 character reference. A man cautiously walks down a dark, narrow corridor. He holds a flashlight, its beam cutting through the darkness. The camera follows closely behind him, handheld. The only sound is his footsteps and nervous breathing.`

---

## 4. 音乐视频

**目标：** 与音轨同步的、视觉上富有动感的片段。

```
// Beat Sync Template
@Image1 artist, @Audio1 track. The artist performs in a [location]. Quick cuts sync to the drum beat at [BPM] BPM. [Camera moves] on the chorus. [Style].
```

**示例：** `@Image1 artist, @Audio1 track. The artist performs in a neon-lit warehouse. Quick cuts sync to the drum beat at 120 BPM. Whip pans and snap zooms on the chorus. Energetic and vibrant.`

---

## 5. 风光 / 旅行

**目标：** 自然与城市环境的宏大、唯美镜头。

```
// Drone Aerial Template
Sweeping aerial drone shot over [location] at [time of day]. [Weather condition]. The camera slowly [camera move] to reveal the vast scale. [Style].
```

**示例：** `Sweeping aerial drone shot over the Scottish Highlands at dawn. Mist hangs low in the valleys. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the vast scale of the mountains. Epic and majestic.`

---

## 6. 商业 / 品牌

**目标：** 唤起特定品牌感的精致高端视觉。

```
// Car Ad Template
A sleek [color] [vehicle type] drives on a [road type] at [time of day]. The camera tracks alongside the car, [camera position]. Lens flare from the sun. The car looks [brand feeling].
```

**示例：** `A sleek black sports car drives on a winding mountain road at sunset. The camera tracks alongside the car, low to the ground. Lens flare from the sun. The car looks powerful and luxurious.`

---

## 7. 动漫 / 艺术

**目标：** 实现特定的、非写实的视觉风格。

```
// Cel-Shaded Action Template
An anime [character type] with [features], in the style of [studio/artist]. They [action]. Dynamic [camera angle]. Bold outlines, flat color fills, high-contrast shading. [Energy].
```

**示例：** `An anime warrior with white hair, with bold ink outlines, flat color fills, high-contrast cel shading, and smear frames on fast motion. He draws a glowing blue sword. Dynamic low-angle shot. Bold outlines, flat color fills, high-contrast shading. Explosive energy.`
