# Style Intelligence Ledger

Purpose: preserve reusable aesthetic style intelligence for companion skills. Keep entries compact, evidence-backed, and portable.

## Entry States

- `candidate`: interesting but still single-source, fragile, or untested.
- `tracked`: repeated across credible sources or useful in one successful task.
- `stable`: repeated, bounded, and portable across at least two contexts.
- `deprecated`: overused, misleading, visually unstable, or no longer useful.

## Daily Scan Protocol

Use each scan to compare sources across at least four lanes when possible:

- film / cinematography / music video
- animation / illustration / comics
- 3D / game cinematic / motion design
- fashion / product / architecture / interiors
- graphic design / typography / editorial / UI
- AI image/video communities and model behavior reports

Prioritize primary sources and professional criticism. Treat reposted moodboards, viral terms, and prompt packs as weak evidence unless they lead to inspectable originals.
