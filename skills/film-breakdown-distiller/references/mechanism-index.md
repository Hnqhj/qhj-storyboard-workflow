# Mechanism Index

Compact index for `mechanism-bank.md`. Use this first to choose relevant precedents and avoid duplicate mechanisms.

## Subjective Perception And Emotional Access

- `Avoidance-POV Reaction` - subjective camera shows emotion through what attention avoids and reluctantly returns to. Example: `Nickel Boys`.
- `Voice-Image Distance Field` - intimate voice over impersonal images creates absence, exile, or unreturned address. Example: `News from Home`.
- `Chromatic Empathy Contamination` - recurring color fields and mediated surfaces show dangerous investigative identification. Example: `Manhunter`.
- `Interrogative Blank Infection` - repeated questions, pauses, and ordinary cues make influence feel self-generated. Example: `Cure`.
- `Acousmatic Impulse Breach` - unseen sound enters routine and turns familiar gestures into irreversible thresholds. Example: `Chime`.

## Sound, Evidence, And Reconstruction

- `Parallel Offscreen Sonic World` - unseen space pressures calm images through mapped, recurring sound. Example: `The Zone of Interest`.
- `Recorded-Sound Reconstruction Trap` - replayed evidence changes meaning through filtering, emphasis, and listener interpretation. Example: `The Conversation`.
- `Tactile Sound Procedure` - small material sounds map risk, progress, and detection thresholds. Example: `A Man Escaped`.
- `Team Silence Choreography` - silence reveals group roles, handoffs, and risk discipline. Example: `Rififi`.

## Routine, Gesture, And Object Systems

- `Routine-Deviation Pressure` - repeated procedure turns tiny deviations into drama. Example: `Jeanne Dielman`.
- `Hand-Fragment Ellipsis` - hands, objects, and glances let the viewer reconstruct covert action. Example: `Pickpocket`.
- `Object-Causality Relay` - transferred objects carry pressure and consequence across people. Example: `L'Argent`.
- `Gesture-Code Compression` - professional identity is expressed through repeated ritual, wardrobe, and controlled gesture. Example: `Le Samourai`.
- `Pre-Action Ritual Drag` - preparation and role-readiness outweigh execution, cooling suspense into fatalism. Example: `Un Flic`.

## Space, Landscape, And Social Pressure

- `Lateral-Waiting Witness` - slow lateral observation turns waiting faces and public space into evidence. Example: `D'Est`.
- `Policy-Landscape Pressure` - testimony plus infrastructure makes policy visible as spatial risk. Example: `From the Other Side`.
- `Deferred-Catastrophe Grief Lattice` - nonlinear aftermath lets testimony, memory, law, myth, and withheld disaster pressure one another. Example: `The Sweet Hereafter`.

## Procedural Suspense And Heist Geometry

- `Endurance-Procedure Suspense` - extended manual labor makes time, trust, and detection physically felt. Example: `Le Trou`.
- `Convergent-Expertise Geometry` - specialists become a temporary system through role slots, routes, and timing. Example: `Le Cercle Rouge`.

## Vehicles, Roads, And Moving Threats

- `Fragile-Load Geography` - volatile cargo turns every road texture, stop, slope, and vehicle sound into a threshold. Example: `The Wages of Fear`.
- `Dynamic-Obstacle Threshold` - unstable passage, weather, vehicle response, and fragile load become an active obstacle system. Example: `Sorcerer`.
- `Faceless-Machine Antagonist` - vehicle body, sound, and road behavior replace visible human psychology. Example: `Duel`.
- `Trust-System Contamination` - a motive-void stranger corrupts roads, police, diners, evidence, and refuge systems. Example: `The Hitcher`.

## Action, Combat, And Attrition

- `Centerline Melee Escalation` - dense multi-fighter action stays readable through a stable combat center and one-variable escalation. Example: `The Furious`.
- `Corridor-Pressure Weapon Recycling` - narrow linear space turns bodies, surfaces, and reused weapons into escalating compression. Example: `Kill`.
- `Vertical Attrition Gauntlet` - floors, stairwells, corridors, rooms, and dwindling resources make a building an escalation machine. Example: `The Raid: Redemption`.

## Investigation, Time, And Obsession

- `Case-File Time Erosion` - dates, documents, interviews, dead ends, and aging spaces make investigation feel life-consuming. Example: `Zodiac`.

## Selection Heuristic

- For AI-video prompt transfer, prefer mechanisms with visible/audible review checks.
- For subtle drama, search perception, sound, routine, and aftermath mechanisms first.
- For action staging, search vehicle, combat, and procedural geometry mechanisms first.
- When two mechanisms overlap, pair them as validation examples instead of adding a third near-duplicate.
