## Retained

- The effect lifecycle must be continuous and causal rather than a list of nouns.
- Source, carrier, path, spatial depth, interaction, peak, residue, and endpoint are separate decisions.
- One dominant family and a small set of job-assigned atoms are more reliable than synonym stacking.
- Particle systems require emitter, distribution, motion, speed curve, interaction, lifetime, and disappearance.
- Image prompts freeze one phase; video prompts express state change.
- Sound, light, material, camera, and environment must respond to the same event.
- A useful ending preserves a changed physical state or residue.

## Rejected or Demoted

- “More particles,” “stronger glow,” “full-screen flash,” “ultimate,” or “epic” are not treated as mechanisms.
- Fixed 0–3/3–7/7–11/11–15 timing is not mandatory; it remains an optional structural template.
- Generic charge -> explosion is not forced on every effect.
- Full-frame glow, shake, or particle density is not used to repair missing action causality.
- The new skill is not a duplicate general VFX owner. It is the construction and vocabulary specialist under `cinematic-vfx-director` and before `seedance-vfx`.

## Maintenance Rule

When adding terms, add only atoms that create a distinct visible decision. Do not add a synonym unless its geometry, material, motion, or operation differs. Update the search bank and rerun the skill validator after meaningful changes.
