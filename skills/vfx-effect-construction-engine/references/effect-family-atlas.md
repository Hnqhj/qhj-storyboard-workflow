## 1. Selection Rule

Choose the family that best proves the shot's main information. A family is a material-and-motion system, not a color label.

```text
desired proof -> dominant family -> carrier/material -> spatial operation -> residue
```

## 3. Ownership Grammar

Give each recurring faction, character, weapon, or system a stable effect DNA:

```text
source location + carrier material + primary geometry + motion law +
color/value ownership + sound family + residue signature
```

Examples of distinct systems:

- a ritual sword may use ink-dark fracture lines, paper-fiber ash, directional crescent cuts, dry bell transients, and seals that cool into charcoal marks;
- a reactor weapon may use seam-origin plasma, radial circuit activation, refractive pressure cones, capacitor whine, and cooling metal afterglow;
- a living curse may use wet membrane growth, asymmetric crawling paths, absorbed environmental color, viscous sound, and fibrous residue.

Do not make different powers interchangeable by giving all of them the same glowing ring and particle cloud.

## 4. Combination Rules

Use one dominant family and one support family when necessary:

| Dominant | Valid support | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| ritual | particles | reveal field direction or residue, not decoration |
| weapon motion | shockwave | prove selected heavy contact |
| elemental | atmosphere | show propagation through rain, fog, dust, or water |
| technology | color/glow | show state, charge, warning, or ownership |
| space-time | debris | provide scale, direction, and parallax reference |
| transformation | organic or energy | define the material process of the active frontier |

Reject combinations that duplicate the same job or compete at the same visual scale.

## 5. Intensity Grading

### Minor

- local wake, cloth/hair snap, edge streak, short transient;
- no global exposure change;
- fast decay.

### Medium

- clear local geometry or field;
- receiver displacement or local material response;
- short environment and camera response.

### Hero

- selective anticipation contrast;
- exact contact or operation remains readable;
- one decisive shape and environment-scale consequence;
- strong receiver acknowledgment;
- clear decay and changed endpoint.

Optional numeric percentages are control hypotheses, not guaranteed physical parameters. Use them only for A/B testing one dimension such as density, deformation, aggression, or expression while keeping the mechanism unchanged.
